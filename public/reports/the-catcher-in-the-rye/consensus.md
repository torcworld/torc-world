## 4. Consensus analysis

### A. Operational Order rationale

The recurring argument across the reports is that symbolic representation alone does not adequately explain the completed novel. Holden's consciousness is not merely the viewpoint from which alienation is depicted; his own interpretive categories become part of the problem being investigated.

Across the evaluations, the most consistently identified structure is Holden's attempt to divide reality into authenticity and “phoniness,” innocence and corruption, childhood and adulthood. That classificatory system repeatedly becomes unstable because Holden himself lies, performs identities, desires contact while sabotaging it, condemns behavior he also exhibits, and cannot make even valued relationships conform to the purity he demands.

The reports therefore converge on a deeper contradiction: Holden seeks permanence, innocence, authenticity, protection, and secure attachment in a reality governed by change, maturation, sexuality, mortality, separation, compromise, and vulnerability. Allie's death is repeatedly treated as especially important because it prevents the conflict from being reduced to defective schools, hypocritical adults, or corrupt social arrangements.

The attached evaluators express the same structure explicitly: Holden's demand for permanence and innocence conflicts with a reality constituted by change and mortality, making consciousness itself implicated in the fracture. Another report likewise identifies the governing problem as the impossibility of stabilizing Holden's own orientation toward reality rather than merely exposing social “phoniness.”

That recurring reasoning warrants **3b**: the dominant operation is an existential confrontation between consciousness and reality that cannot simply be corrected externally.

### B. Boundary analysis

#### Why 3b rather than 3a

The reports consistently acknowledge that a 3a reading has substantial local evidence. Holden attacks prep schools, status behavior, Hollywood, conventional adulthood, social etiquette, hypocrisy, sexuality, and other forms of “phoniness.”

But six evaluators explicitly argue that correction of those external conditions would not resolve the governing conflict. Allie would remain dead; Phoebe would continue to grow; childhood would remain impermanent; intimacy would continue to involve vulnerability; and Holden himself would remain implicated in behaviors he condemns.

This is the repeated **Existential Resistance** argument. When period setting, slang, schools, social conventions, and particular symbolic devices are stripped away, the deeper structure remains: a consciousness demanding permanence, purity, and protected attachment must inhabit an existence defined by irreversible change and loss.

Accordingly, the fracture is existential rather than primarily corrective.

#### Why 3b rather than 3c

This is the only material TORC dispute.

All seven reports recognize that the carousel sequence changes something. Holden permits Phoebe to reach for the gold ring despite the possibility of falling, and his final admission that he misses people complicates the defensive separation between himself and the people he has judged.

The **six-evaluator majority** concludes that this movement remains tentative. Holden does not establish a stable orientation capable of integrating adulthood, mortality, ambiguity, intimacy, change, and loss. The ending therefore represents an opening toward integration rather than integrated recursive consciousness itself. One attached evaluator describes the endpoint as “a slight opening toward integration rather than an achieved integrated structure.” Another similarly concludes that the carousel produces genuine movement but not a sufficiently developed coherent existential orientation.

The **3c minority evaluator** gives the carousel greater structural force. On that reading, Holden's governing relation changes from **protection as preservation from change** to **protection as remaining present while permitting change and risk**. Because the contradiction remains rather than disappearing, that evaluator considers this a modest but real integration.

The consensus follows the majority because six independent reports judge that the novel preserves and exposes the contradiction more decisively than it integrates it. The **3c interpretation remains the principal minority position and the principal uncertainty**, rather than being discarded.

There is no material G dispute. Reports addressing the issue agree that no independently identifiable target domain receives a new generative explanatory mechanism whose explanatory foundation replaces the recursive-existential account. The novel's achievement remains adequately explained within Level 3.

## 5. Six-dimensional synthesis

### RL — Relational Load

**Consensus result: 6.81**
**Median: 6.4**
**Observed range: 6.4–7.5**

All evaluators identify a substantial nonlocal network involving Holden's narration, Allie's death, Phoebe, “phoniness,” childhood innocence, sexuality, loneliness, the museum, ducks, catcher fantasy, failed encounters, and carousel sequence. Earlier behavior repeatedly acquires additional significance when these elements are related to later disclosures.

The disagreement concerns **degree of non-decomposability**, not the presence of meaningful relations. Higher-scoring evaluators regard the distributed motifs and psychological relations as sufficiently constraining to approach advanced dependency. Lower-scoring evaluators emphasize that many individual episodes remain locally intelligible or substitutable without requiring simultaneous reconstruction of the entire network. The attached reports capture this lower-anchor rationale directly.

The resulting consensus is therefore substantial relational coordination with meaningful nonlocal dependency, but uncertainty over whether that dependency is dense enough for the upper-7 range.

### AD — Abstraction Depth

**Consensus result: 6.57**
**Median: 6.3**
**Observed range: 5.8–7.3**

Evaluators consistently identify movement from concrete encounters toward broader structures involving authenticity, innocence, permanence, change, mortality, identity, social performance, memory, attachment, and representation. A further layer arises because Holden's descriptions of reality simultaneously become evidence about the consciousness producing those descriptions.

The principal disagreement is how far that recursive feature should raise the abstraction score. The high reports treat the narrator-as-object structure as advanced meta-representation. The lower reports regard the abstraction as strong but still concentrated around one consciousness and one closely related existential field rather than several deeply coordinated abstract systems. One attached evaluation accordingly scores the movement from concrete experience through “phoniness” and adulthood toward the consciousness/change conflict below the advanced-7 threshold.

### CD — Constraint Density

**Consensus result: 6.63**
**Median: 6.5**
**Observed range: 5.8–7.4**

The reports repeatedly identify coupled construction requirements: Holden must remain distinctive in voice, psychologically consistent, limited in self-understanding, unreliable without becoming unintelligible, abrasive yet sympathetic, comic and grief-stricken, resistant to maturation yet capable of limited movement. Recurring motifs must operate naturally within that voice while accumulating structural significance.

The disagreement concerns how narrowly these demands constrain the viable construction space. Higher scorers emphasize how many functions individual scenes and stylistic decisions perform simultaneously. Lower scorers note that many encounters, locations, secondary characters, and episode arrangements could be changed while preserving the central achievement. The evaluator range of **1.6 points** is tied directly to this difference between **multiple coupled constraints** and **truly narrow solution-space architecture**.

### ID — Inferential Distance

**Consensus result: 6.19**
**Median: 6.1**
**Observed range: 5.4–6.8**

All seven evaluations agree that the novel's deepest structure is not simply stated by Holden. Readers must reconstruct relations among grief, Allie's death, innocence, “phoniness,” sexuality, loneliness, defensive judgment, failed connection, the museum, ducks, catcher fantasy, and carousel.

ID is nevertheless consistently one of the lower dimensions. The central reason is also consistent: repeated motifs and converging narrative cues progressively scaffold the reconstruction. The leap from Holden's explicit account to the deeper psychological/existential architecture is non-routine, but not exceptionally remote or radically underdetermined. One evaluator summarizes the bottleneck as substantial reconstruction that is “deliberately recoverable from repeated cues.”

The disagreement is mainly calibration—whether this warrants the mid-5s or upper-6s—rather than disagreement about the inferential operation itself.

### IC — Integrative Compression

**Consensus result: 7.20**
**Median: 6.8**
**Observed range: 6.6–8.0**

This is the clearest cross-evaluator strength and the **highest mean dimension**.

Across the reports, a comparatively compact architecture—Holden's narrative voice together with a limited family of recurring motifs and oppositions—organizes grief, mortality, sexuality, class, childhood, adulthood, authenticity, social performance, loneliness, memory, maturation, attachment, and resistance to change.

The strongest common claim is therefore one of cognitive economy: relatively few recurring structures carry substantially more psychological and existential information than their surface simplicity would suggest. The attached reports repeatedly identify IC as the profile's strongest feature.
The remaining disagreement concerns scale. High evaluators judge the compact architecture sufficient for approximately IC8; lower evaluators accept strong compression but regard the heterogeneous load and reconstructive scope as insufficient for the complete advanced anchor.

### GR — Generalization Reach

**Consensus result: 6.26**
**Median: 6.4**
**Observed range: 5.4–7.0**

The evaluations agree that the governing structure transfers beyond Holden's exact biography and setting. They repeatedly identify portable patterns involving grief, defensive alienation, idealized innocence, authenticity judgments, resistance to irreversible change, maturation, compromised social participation, and protective attachment.

GR nevertheless produces one of the two largest ranges, **1.6 points**. The dividing issue is how strictly to interpret transfer beyond human psychological and developmental contexts. Higher evaluators award stronger transfer because the architecture remains recognizable across varied human situations. Lower evaluators invoke the protocol's firewall against treating broad human resonance as equivalent to structurally faithful transfer across markedly different problem-spaces. One attached report explicitly identifies that limitation: the architecture transfers across broad developmental and existential cases but remains principally inside the human psychological/social ontology.

The consensus therefore supports meaningful generalization, but not extreme far-domain reach.

# Consensus Evaluation

*The Catcher in the Rye* receives a consensus classification of **Level 3b — Fractured Recursive Consciousness**, with a headline median CMS of **638** and a mean CMS of **661**.

The operational classification is substantially more stable than the magnitude score. Six of seven independent evaluators place the novel at 3b, and their reasoning converges on the same structural feature: Holden's consciousness is not merely the vehicle through which alienation is represented but becomes itself an object of recursive disclosure. His governing distinctions—authentic versus “phony,” innocent versus corrupted, child versus adult—repeatedly fail to stabilize either the world or his own conduct. The novel progressively reveals that the interpretive consciousness accusing reality of falseness is itself implicated in the contradiction it encounters.

The recurring center of that contradiction is Holden's demand for permanence, innocence, authentic connection, and protection in a reality governed by change, maturation, sexuality, mortality, separation, compromise, and vulnerability. This is why the consensus exceeds a corrective 3a interpretation. Social hypocrisy, institutional absurdity, status performance, and adult convention matter, but removing or repairing them would not eliminate the governing crisis. Allie's death remains irreversible, Phoebe must continue to grow, intimacy remains risky, and Holden cannot preserve childhood against the conditions of finite existence.

The principal TORC uncertainty lies one level higher. Every evaluator recognizes the carousel as the strongest evidence for 3c. Holden's willingness to let Phoebe reach for the gold ring marks a genuine modification of his preservation fantasy, and his final admission that he misses people weakens the defensive opposition between himself and the “phony” world. The minority 3c evaluation interprets this as a structural transformation: protection no longer means preventing the fall but remaining present while allowing risk and change. The six-evaluator majority, however, concludes that the insight remains too local and tentative to establish a durable existential orientation capable of containing mortality, maturation, ambiguity, attachment, and loss. On that evidence, the completed artifact moves toward integration without making integration its dominant operation. The consensus therefore remains **3b**.

The magnitude profile is less stable because evaluators differ substantially in anchor calibration. Three assessments fall between CMS 710 and 725, while four fall between 607 and 638. Yet underneath that numerical division lies a recognizable architectural consensus. The novel's strongest capacity is **Integrative Compression (mean 7.20)**: a compact mechanism consisting primarily of Holden's voice and a limited network of recurring motifs organizes a broad psychological and existential field without reducing its internal distinctions. **Relational Load, Abstraction Depth, and Constraint Density** form the next tier, reflecting the distributed interaction among grief, innocence, sexuality, authenticity, memory, social performance, attachment, and Holden's limited self-understanding.

**Inferential Distance** is more restrained because the reader's reconstruction, while indirect and non-routine, is progressively scaffolded by repeated converging cues. **Generalization Reach** is similarly moderated: the architecture transfers strongly across experiences of grief, adolescence, alienation, defensive idealization, maturation, and resistance to change, but evaluators disagree about how much credit should be given for transfer that remains principally within human psychological and existential contexts.

The resulting consensus profile is therefore best understood as **3b | 638**: a work whose demonstrated intelligence lies chiefly in compactly coordinating a psychologically rich recursive fracture rather than in extreme inferential remoteness, far-domain generalization, or generative explanatory replacement. Its most consequential achievement is the conversion of a seemingly simple adolescent vocabulary of “phoniness,” innocence, falling, disappearance, and preservation into a sustained confrontation between consciousness's demand for a world that will not change and an existence in which change cannot be escaped.
