## 1. Operational Order (TORC)

**Level 3c — Integrated Recursive Consciousness**

The lowest sufficient classification for *Ulysses* is **3c**. Its dominant global operation is not merely symbolic organization of a day in Dublin, nor merely the representation of fractured consciousness. The novel repeatedly makes **representation, interpretation, memory, language, perspective, and the consciousness–reality relation themselves objects of the work**, then integrates their instability into a larger coherent structure without eliminating that instability.

This is especially visible in the way radically different representational modes remain structurally coordinated: Stephen’s abstract and self-questioning consciousness, Bloom’s embodied associative cognition, Molly’s concluding monologue, the mythic correspondences, recurring motifs and memories, Dublin’s physical topology, and episodes whose governing styles themselves become part of what is being investigated. “Proteus,” “Sirens,” “Oxen of the Sun,” “Circe,” “Ithaca,” and “Penelope” do not merely decorate represented reality with symbolism; they repeatedly alter the conditions under which reality is apprehended. Under the protocol, Level 3 begins precisely when representation or the conditions of intelligibility become the dominant object of inquiry rather than symbolism remaining sufficient. 

The novel then goes beyond 3b. Its contradictions—alienation and social connection, fidelity and betrayal, mortality and continuance, bodily materiality and abstraction, unstable language and persistent meaning, estrangement and homecoming—are not finally removed. Yet the completed work incorporates them into a coherent existential orientation centered on continued ordinary existence, plurality of consciousness, relational connection, memory, embodiment, and recurrence. That is close to the protocol’s defining 3c operation: contradiction remains operative while consciousness develops a structure capable of containing it. 

### Boundary analysis

**Why 3c rather than 3b:** 3b would be sufficient if the completed work primarily *inhabited* irresolvable fracture. *Ulysses* certainly performs that operation locally and extensively, but its completed architecture does something additional. Bloom’s return, the Bloom–Stephen encounter and separation, the catechistic reconstruction of domestic reality in “Ithaca,” and especially the transition into Molly’s radically different consciousness do not erase contradiction; they absorb incompatible perspectives into an encompassing but non-totalizing human order. That meets the protocol’s distinction between mere confrontation and genuine integration. 

**Why not G:** the strongest candidate G reading would say that *Ulysses* relocates the explanation of human experience from an externally narrated world to the linguistic, embodied, associative processes through which consciousness constructs intelligibility. That is a serious candidate, but the protocol sets a much stricter threshold. G requires an **independently identifiable explanatory target**, a **generative explanatory mechanism**, and an actual **replacement of the previously sufficient explanatory foundation**; radical representational method or artifact-internal semantic generation is explicitly insufficient.  

Applied directly:

- **Independent target:** human consciousness/ordinary experience can be stated independently of the novel, so this preliminary requirement is available. 
- **Previously unseen generative structure:** the novel demonstrates highly original structures for representing cognition and meaning-making, but what it establishes most securely is a representational/recursive architecture rather than a mechanism explaining why independently existing forms of consciousness take the forms they do. 
- **Explanatory relocation:** not sufficiently established. The novel transforms how consciousness can be represented and recursively investigated, but the protocol forbids treating a transformation of representation itself as equivalent to changing *what explains* the target. 
- **Generative mechanism vs explanatory schema:** its stylistic and perspectival systems organize and disclose cognition with extraordinary power, but they do not repeatedly generate nontrivial explanatory consequences across independent instances of consciousness in the stronger protocol sense. 
- **Explanatory replacement:** a 3c account remains sufficient to explain the completed artifact. Therefore the prior level has not been rendered operationally inadequate. 

Accordingly, G fails. This is exactly the sort of case the protocol warns against inflating on the basis of complexity, formal novelty, recursive structure, or generative-looking internal machinery.

**RL — 9.1**

The complete **9 anchor** is warranted: the novel sustains exceptional heterogeneous interdependence across character consciousnesses, physical geography, chronology, recurring objects and motifs, bodily processes, mythic correspondences, linguistic registers, cultural references, memory networks, and episode-level formal systems. Remote elements frequently alter the significance of one another, and interpretation of the whole is materially weakened by modular decomposition. That satisfies the protocol’s 9-anchor requirement of interdependence spanning substantially different levels or domains. 

There is limited movement toward 10 because the relational span is unusually extreme, but full RL10 is not established: several episodes retain substantial local autonomy, and not every component is so mutually constraining that removal or alteration would collapse the global relational achievement. **Principal evidence:** recurrent Bloom/Stephen/Molly relations; Dublin topology; Homeric correspondences; cross-episode motifs; stylistic structures whose local meaning depends on the completed whole.

**AD — 8.8**

The **8 anchor** is fully satisfied: *Ulysses* traverses substantial representational distance while preserving disciplined structural correspondence—from concrete perception to inner speech, memory, linguistic form, cultural history, mythic schema, narrative technique, and reflection on representation itself. It approaches 9 because these layers interact as meta-representational structures across highly heterogeneous material. 

I stop below 9 because the abstraction architecture does not consistently function as a single very deep invariant system governing the entire heterogeneous field; substantial portions remain irreducibly particular, sensory, dramatic, or perspectival rather than being subordinated to one exceptionally deep meta-structure. **Principal evidence:** “Proteus,” “Oxen of the Sun,” “Ithaca,” the Homeric scaffolding, and systematic changes in narrative representation.

**CD — 8.6**

The **8 anchor** is fully demonstrated. Numerous heterogeneous requirements interact across levels: one-day chronology, spatial continuity, character histories, recurrent motifs, stylistic constraints peculiar to episodes, mythic parallels, linguistic register, bodily realism, and cumulative thematic relations. Local formal choices regularly interact with or compromise other requirements. 

Movement toward 9 is substantial, but the 9 anchor requires a *very narrow class* of successful constructions. The novel’s central achievement could survive many local substitutions, deletions, or rearrangements; its density is therefore not equivalent to near-unique solution-space compression. This is important because the protocol explicitly warns against mistaking large-narrative complexity for extreme constraint density. **Principal evidence:** the simultaneous coordination of time, location, recurring motifs, stylistic programs, character continuity, and intertextual structure.

**ID — 8.7**

The **8 anchor** is fully warranted: the construction requires multiple highly non-obvious restructurings for which no routine procedure supplies the route—the equation of epic structure with ordinary metropolitan life, radical shifts in narrative technique as cognitive instruments, the systematic fusion of interior association with external detail, and the transformation of linguistic history into narrative form. 

It approaches 9 because the successful architecture remains deeply underdetermined by its available materials; there are innumerable plausible ways to combine Dublin, consciousness, myth, language history, and quotidian experience, and the achieved relations are far from obvious. I remain below 9 because artifact-only evidence cannot establish quite strongly enough the protocol’s counterfactual that even a strong domain reasoner would find the successful reconstruction exceptionally underdetermined. **Principal evidence:** the episode-specific formal inventions and their integration into one continuous narrative system.

**IC — 8.7**

The **8 anchor** is fully demonstrated: a comparatively small set of governing architectures—the single day, three central consciousnesses, recurrent Dublin geography, bodily cycles, mythic correspondences, and patterned motifs—organizes an enormous heterogeneous field while preserving fine local distinctions and enabling extensive reconstruction of relations among episodes. 

It moves strongly toward 9 because the load being integrated is immense, but it falls short of the 9 anchor’s “remarkably economical architecture.” *Ulysses* is integrative, but some of its complexity remains explicitly elaborated rather than strongly compressed; its governing architecture does not reduce the heterogeneous information load with quite the economy required for exceptional compression. The protocol specifically forbids treating sheer integrated complexity as compression.  **Principal evidence:** the one-day framework, recurring character network, Homeric scaffolding, thematic recurrence, and episode-to-episode stylistic architecture.

**GR — 7.7**

The **7 anchor** is fully demonstrated. The novel’s underlying structures—perspectival cognition, associative interiority, interaction of bodily experience with abstract thought, linguistic mediation of reality, parallel organization of ordinary and archetypal experience—transfer convincingly across Stephen, Bloom, Molly, and many subsidiary consciousnesses and social situations. The structure has strong fidelity across substantially varied human cases. 

It moves substantially toward 8 because those structures encompass heterogeneous psychological, social, linguistic, sexual, religious, familial, and material situations. I stop below 8 because the protocol requires transfer across **markedly heterogeneous problem-spaces**, not merely very broad relevance within human experience; it explicitly warns that broad human/social applicability cannot automatically be treated as extreme domain distance.  **Principal evidence:** recurrence of the same consciousness/representation architecture across markedly different characters, registers, social roles, and experiential situations.
