## Operational Order (TORC): **Level 3c — Integrated Recursive Consciousness**

I classify *Ulysses* at **3c**, not because of its canonical reputation or formal difficulty, but because the completed novel's dominant operation is recursive: it repeatedly makes the processes by which consciousness represents, interprets, remembers, linguistically constructs, and inhabits reality into the material of the work itself. Symbolic organization alone is therefore insufficient. Under the protocol, Level 3 begins precisely when representation, interpretation, orientation, or the conditions under which reality becomes intelligible themselves become objects of inquiry. 

More specifically, the novel does not merely confront fragmentation. Across Bloom, Stephen, Molly, the Dublin environment, bodily existence, memory, grief, sexuality, nationality, language, and radically changing representational modes, it constructs a larger order capable of *containing* incompatible perspectives and unresolved contradictions without eliminating them. That is the distinctive 3c operation: preservation of fracture coupled with coherent integration, rather than simple reconciliation or closure. The protocol explicitly distinguishes integration from resolution and requires contradiction to remain operative inside the resulting orientation.  

### Boundary analysis

**Why 3c rather than 3b.** A 3b account captures a great deal of the novel: unstable identity, mortality, estrangement, failed communication, competing systems of meaning, psychological fragmentation, and the inability of consciousness to obtain a final reconciliation with existence. But 3b requires the dominant movement to remain confrontation rather than integration.  That is insufficient for the completed novel.

The decisive evidence is global rather than any single episode. The many stylistic consciousnesses do not simply fracture the work into incompatible pieces. They remain coordinated by the single day, Dublin's shared physical world, intersecting trajectories, recurring memories and motifs, bodily cycles, parallel situations, and the Bloom–Stephen–Molly relational structure. The later portions do not abolish death, infidelity, alienation, failed paternity/filiality, or epistemic uncertainty; instead those remain inside a larger structure in which ordinary embodied existence can continue. Molly's concluding movement is especially important not as "optimistic closure," but because affirmation occurs while contradiction, memory, infidelity, resentment, desire, mortality, and contingency remain intact. This meets the protocol's Integration Resistance Principle more closely than a 3b terminal confrontation.

**Why not G.** This is the crucial ceiling. The protocol explicitly forbids inferring G from complexity, originality, recursion, abstraction, formal virtuosity, or artifact-internal generativity. G requires **explanatory relocation**: a newly disclosed generative structure must explain an independently identifiable target and make the previously sufficient explanatory basis inadequate. 

Applied explicitly:

- **Independent Target Test:** plausible targets can be named—human consciousness, linguistic mediation, urban experience, identity—but this alone does not establish G. 
- **Generative mechanism vs. explanatory schema:** the novel extraordinarily reorganizes those phenomena through shifting styles, perspectives, correspondences, and representational systems. But these principally *instantiate and investigate* mediation rather than establish a generative mechanism explaining why independently existing instances of consciousness or social reality take the forms they do. 
- **Explanatory Replacement Principle:** no lower-order account becomes inadequate in the required G sense. Integrated recursive consciousness still explains what the completed novel globally accomplishes. 
- **Explanatory-Object Firewall:** transforming how consciousness is represented is not automatically transforming what explains consciousness. 
- **Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall:** the extraordinary capacity of each episode's stylistic system to generate its own semantic world is precisely the kind of internal generativity the protocol says is insufficient for G unless it relocates explanation for an independent target. 
- **Levels 1–3 insufficiency:** fails. Level 3c remains sufficient. 

The protocol is unusually explicit here: a large or sophisticated representational architecture cannot become G simply by generating its own meanings and forms.  Thus *Ulysses* is an extremely high-magnitude **3c**, not G.

**RL — 9.2**

The complete 9 anchor requires "exceptional heterogeneous interdependence": consequential relations spanning substantially different levels/domains while remaining mutually consequential.  *Ulysses* satisfies this. Character trajectories, geography, clock-time, bodily processes, memory, historical reference, recurring motifs, linguistic registers, episode-specific styles, Homeric correspondences, social relationships, and perspective continually constrain and reinterpret one another across remote portions of the novel.

The movement beyond 9 comes from the unusual span of that interdependence: microscopic lexical relations coexist with episode-scale formal systems and whole-novel temporal/spatial architecture. I stop at **9.2**, however, because the protocol's 10 requirement demands both *extreme* interdependence and exceptional span; significant portions of individual episodes retain enough local autonomy that complete extreme global coupling is not established.

**Principal evidence:** cross-episode motifs; synchronized Dublin trajectories; Bloom/Stephen/Molly relations; stylistic systems interacting with subject matter; distant textual recurrences; simultaneous linguistic, psychological, spatial, temporal, bodily, historical, and mythic organization.

**AD — 8.8**

The 8 anchor requires deep constrained abstraction with substantial conceptual/representational distance and preserved structural correspondence; 9 requires very deep invariant/meta-level structures organizing broad heterogeneous material with unusually disciplined relation-preservation. 

The novel repeatedly moves from concrete sensation → thought → memory → language → cultural forms → the representation of consciousness → transformations in the representational system itself. Its successive styles can make not merely events but the *conditions and conventions of representing events* perceptible. That approaches the 9 anchor strongly. I keep it at **8.8** because its deepest meta-representational structures remain plural, exploratory, and partly local rather than being governed by a sufficiently stable invariant architecture to establish the full 9 anchor.

**Principal evidence:** nested consciousness; representation becoming an object of representation; systematic stylistic transformations; relations between immediate sensation, memory, discourse, cultural form, and narrative convention.

**CD — 8.6**

The 8 anchor requires heterogeneous requirements interacting across levels such that local optimization regularly compromises other requirements; 9 requires numerous strongly coupled constraints leaving a *very narrow* class of successful constructions. 

The novel simultaneously maintains chronology, geography, character continuity, recurrent motifs, stylistic differentiation, episode-specific formal programs, allusive structures, bodily and psychological plausibility, linguistic register, and whole-work correspondences. Altering one subsystem often damages several others.

But the protocol warns specifically against equating enormous narrative complexity with extreme CD and asks how much could be changed while preserving the central achievement.  Many local details and even some formal correspondences could vary without destroying the central operation. Hence **8.6**, not 9.

**Principal evidence:** simultaneous temporal, spatial, characterological, stylistic, thematic, intertextual, and recursive representational requirements.

**ID — 8.3**

The 8 anchor requires highly non-obvious restructuring with meaningful alternatives and no routine route; 9 requires an exceptional reconstruction where rich available material still leaves the successful relation deeply underdetermined. 

The central inferential/constructive leap is not merely "write stream of consciousness." It is discovering that an ordinary day can be reconstructed through a succession of radically different representational architectures while those transformations themselves expose relations among perception, memory, language, identity, culture, and reality. There is no routine procedure leading from the available narrative materials to that solution.

I assign **8.3** rather than 9 because a creative artifact gives weaker evidence about its originating problem-space than a proof or explicitly documented performance trace. The resulting restructuring is plainly non-routine, but the degree of initial underdetermination cannot be reconstructed confidently enough to establish the exceptional 9 anchor.

**Principal evidence:** transformation of mundane narrative material into a multi-system investigation of consciousness and representation; non-obvious coordination of radically different formal solutions.

**IC — 8.2**

The 8 anchor requires a comparatively compact architecture organizing a large heterogeneous field with strong preservation and considerable reconstructive power. The protocol also warns that sheer integration or length is not compression.  

The compression mechanisms are powerful: one day, one city, a small central character network, recurring bodily/psychological motifs, spatial intersection, and a handful of recurrent structural correspondences organize an enormous quantity of linguistic, cultural, historical, psychological, sexual, religious, political, and mundane information.

It nevertheless remains only modestly above 8 because much of the complexity stays explicitly elaborated rather than becoming recoverable from an exceptionally economical governing structure. The novel is enormously integrative; its compression ratio is somewhat less extreme than its relational load.

**Principal evidence:** single-day architecture, constrained geography, recurring character triad/network, motif recurrence, episode architecture, and repeated micro/macro correspondences.

**GR — 7.0**

The 7 anchor requires the same core architecture to transfer reliably across substantially varied cases with strong fidelity and identifiable boundaries. Extreme GR cannot be inferred merely because an artifact speaks broadly to human experience; the protocol explicitly requires actual structural transfer rather than metaphorical relevance. 

The transferable structure is the recursive representation of experience through multiple interacting perceptual, linguistic, mnemonic, bodily, social, and cultural frames. Within the artifact, that architecture successfully operates across markedly different consciousnesses, social situations, intellectual registers, bodily states, and representational conventions.

I stop at **7.0** because the artifact does not itself demonstrate that this architecture retains comparable power across *markedly heterogeneous external problem-spaces*. Broad applicability to human experience cannot be promoted automatically into GR8–9 under the protocol's Applicability/Transfer Firewall. 

**Principal evidence:** transfer of the representational architecture across characters, episodes, cognitive states, social contexts, and radically different stylistic regimes; limited direct evidence of cross-domain transfer.
