## Operational Order (TORC): **Level 3c — Integrated Recursive Consciousness**

*Ulysses* is best classified at **3c**, not because of its difficulty, stylistic experimentation, canonical reputation, or sheer density, but because its dominant operation makes the relation between consciousness and reality itself an object of sustained representation and then integrates that instability into a coherent existential structure. This is exactly the distinction the protocol requires: Level 3 begins only when symbolic organization is insufficient and representation/orientation itself becomes the object of inquiry; 3c further requires preservation of existential contradiction within a coherent orientation rather than its elimination.  

Across the completed novel, ordinary reality is continually reconstructed through heterogeneous modes of consciousness: perception, memory, fantasy, language, bodily sensation, cultural inheritance, interpersonal inference, mythic correspondence, and stylistic convention. The significance of Dublin on 16 June is therefore not adequately captured by saying that concrete events symbolize larger themes. The book persistently exposes *how* experienced reality is constituted through consciousness and representational form. Its changing styles are operational rather than merely decorative: different representational systems make apparently continuous reality intelligible in different ways.

The completed movement toward Molly Bloom's monologue is especially important. The novel does not cure mortality, estrangement, sexual jealousy, contingency, failed communication, or the instability of self-understanding. Instead, these contradictions remain active while the work nevertheless achieves a form of existential inhabitation and coherence through return, memory, embodiment, domesticity, desire, and affirmation. That satisfies the protocol's central 3c distinction: contradiction is incorporated rather than erased.

### Boundary analysis

**Why 3c rather than 3b.** A 3b classification would capture much of the novel: consciousness repeatedly confronts alienation, mortality, uncertainty, failed correspondence between minds, unstable identity, and the inability of inherited meaning systems to produce complete reconciliation. Under the protocol, however, 3b must remain principally in confrontation, whereas 3c requires the contradiction to survive inside a coherent existential orientation.  The completed structure exceeds confrontation. Bloom's return, his limited accommodation with Molly, and finally Molly's consciousness do not solve the contradictions but place them within a larger experiential whole. Removing that integrative movement would materially change what the completed novel does. Thus 3b is insufficient.

**Why not G.** This is the crucial ceiling. The protocol explicitly warns that metafiction, recursion, abstraction, formal experimentation, conceptual difficulty, and interpretive openness can produce a false appearance of generativity. A genuine G operation must relocate explanation rather than merely produce an extraordinarily sophisticated recursive or representational architecture. 

Applying the G tests:

- **Independent Target Test:** plausible targets can be stated—human consciousness, ordinary lived experience, or the relation between language and experienced reality. 
- **Generative-mechanism test:** the novel demonstrates extraordinarily powerful *representational mechanisms* for rendering those targets, but it does not establish a generative explanatory mechanism explaining why independently constrained instances of consciousness take the forms they do. Its stylistic systems primarily enact and reorganize experience. 
- **Explanatory Replacement Principle:** its recursive architecture does not make a lower-order account of consciousness/existence explanatorily impossible in the protocol's required sense. The work enlarges and transforms representation of experience, but does not establish a newly necessary explanatory foundation for the independently identifiable target. 
- **Explanatory-Object Firewall:** innovations in representing consciousness cannot themselves count as relocation of what *explains* consciousness. The protocol explicitly separates transformation of representation/method from transformation of an explanatory foundation. 
- **Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall:** *Ulysses* generates an extraordinarily elaborate semantic and formal world, but artifact-internal generation does not establish G unless that machinery relocates explanation of an independent target. 

Consequently Levels 1–3 are **not demonstrably insufficient**. G therefore fails. This is precisely the sort of case for which the protocol warns that enormous complexity and recursive sophistication do not accumulate into G.
