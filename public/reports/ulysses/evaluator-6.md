## 1. Operational Order (TORC)

**Level 3c — Integrated Recursive Consciousness**

*Ulysses* is best classified at **3c**. Its dominant operation cannot be adequately explained as direct representation or even extraordinarily sophisticated symbolic organization. Across the completed novel, consciousness, interpretation, memory, language, narrative form, identity, and the act of representing experience repeatedly become objects of the work’s own investigation. That satisfies the protocol’s threshold for recursive consciousness: representation and the conditions under which reality becomes intelligible become the operative object, rather than merely the vehicle, of representation. The protocol explicitly requires symbolic organization to be insufficient before Level 3 can be entered.  

The strongest evidence for **3c rather than 3b** lies in the completed architecture. The novel does not simply expose fractures between consciousness and existence—bereavement, marital estrangement, mortality, religious loss, alienation, failed communication, unstable identity, bodily contingency. It progressively contains those fractures within a larger structure of continued ordinary existence: Bloom's persistence in relation and return, Stephen's unresolved but enlarged encounter with Bloom, and most decisively Molly's final monologue, which incorporates contradiction, sexuality, resentment, memory, mortality, infidelity, and attachment into an affirmative consciousness without making those contradictions disappear. The integration is therefore not equivalent to problem-resolution. That distinction is exactly what the protocol requires for 3c.  

### Boundary analysis

**Why 3c rather than 3b.** Level 3b would be sufficient if the dominant completed operation remained confrontation with irreducible existential contradiction. *Ulysses* certainly performs that operation extensively. But the final organization does more: the contradictions are preserved while being incorporated into a coherent existential/artistic totality. The point is not that Molly's concluding affirmation supplies a cheerful ending; taken alone, that would fail the protocol's Integration Resistance Principle. Rather, the novel's accumulated multiplicity—Bloom/Molly/Stephen, body/intellect, fidelity/infidelity, grief/desire, social belonging/exclusion, mythic structure/ordinary materiality—is made coexist within a structure that does not require any pole to vanish. The protocol defines exactly this distinction as the move from fractured recursion to integrated recursion. 

There is nevertheless a genuine boundary ambiguity here. Stephen does not arrive at stable integration, Bloom's marriage is not repaired, and the novel deliberately preserves polyphony rather than collapsing its perspectives into one doctrine. That prevents an especially emphatic 3c judgment. But 3c does **not** require contradiction to disappear or every consciousness to achieve reconciliation; it requires the completed artifact to construct a coherent orientation capable of containing the contradiction. On balance, the work clears that threshold.

**Why not G.** The protocol makes this boundary unusually strict: exceptional complexity, recursion, formal invention, abstraction, and artistic generativity cannot accumulate into G. G requires **explanatory relocation**—a structure that changes what explains an independently identifiable target and renders a previously sufficient explanatory basis inadequate. 

Applied to *Ulysses*, the candidate high-order structure would be something like its treatment of ordinary consciousness through mythic correspondence, shifting stylistic regimes, linguistic mediation, embodied cognition, and perspectival recursion. That structure is extraordinarily generative *inside the artifact*. But the G tests fail:

- **Independent Target Test:** human consciousness, ordinary experience, linguistic mediation, or modern urban subjectivity can all be named independently of the novel, so an independent target is available. But that only opens the gate; it does not satisfy G. 
- **Generative-mechanism vs. explanatory-schema test:** the novel's architecture reveals and reorganizes relations among those phenomena, but it does not establish a mechanism that explains why otherwise distinct independently constrained phenomena must take the forms they do. Much of its power remains representational, recursive, analogical, and formal. 
- **Explanatory Replacement Principle:** previous explanations of consciousness, language, urban experience, or subjectivity are not made operationally insufficient by a newly established explanatory foundation. The work gives an unprecedented realization of those relations rather than demonstrating that an earlier explanatory basis must be abandoned. 
- **Explanatory-Object Firewall:** radically changing literary representation of consciousness is not identical to changing what explains consciousness itself. 
- **Computational/Procedural Generativity Firewall:** the progression of styles, formal rules, correspondences, transformations, and recursive procedures is cognitively relevant but cannot itself establish G. 
- **Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall:** this is the decisive obstacle. *Ulysses* generates an extraordinarily elaborate semantic and representational world through internal structural mechanisms, but the protocol explicitly says that such artifact-internal generativity remains below G unless it relocates the explanatory foundation of an independent target.    

Thus **3c is the lowest sufficient classification**, while G would require attributing explanatory work to the novel that its completed structure does not compel.
