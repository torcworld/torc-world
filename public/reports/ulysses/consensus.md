## 4. Consensus analysis

### A. Operational Order rationale

The consensus classification is **3c — Integrated Recursive Consciousness**.

Across the reports, the recurring Level-3 argument is that *Ulysses* does more than use consciousness, language, symbolism, and stylistic variation to represent a stable world. Different systems of perception, memory, discourse, embodiment, cultural inheritance, perspective, and narrative form continually reconstruct what experienced reality is. Representation itself consequently becomes part of the artifact's object of investigation.

Six evaluators then identify an additional completed operation beyond fractured recursion. The existential and epistemic contradictions remain operative—mortality, estrangement, infidelity, imperfect communication, unstable identity, contingency, representational instability—but are incorporated into an encompassing orientation involving continued embodied existence, relation, memory, recurrence, domestic return, plurality of consciousness, and affirmation. The majority does not claim that these contradictions are solved; its 3c argument depends precisely on their survival inside the larger architecture.

Accordingly, the majority finds 3b insufficient to account for the completed artifact. The sole dissent is substantive but narrowly located at the question of whether this integration is genuinely global and operationally dominant.

### B. Boundary analysis

**Why the artifact satisfies 3c:** Six evaluators find that the work does not terminate in recursive fracture. Its multiple consciousnesses and representational modes remain coordinated within a completed structure that can preserve incompatible perspectives and existential contradiction without forcing reconciliation. Bloom's return and continued relational orientation, Stephen's encounter and departure, and Molly's concluding consciousness recur as the principal evidence.

**Why it does not satisfy G:** Here agreement is effectively complete. The reports distinguish between an extraordinarily generative representational architecture and the stronger G requirement of explanatory relocation. *Ulysses* transforms how consciousness and experience can be represented, organized, and recursively investigated, but the evaluators do not find a newly established generative mechanism that replaces the explanatory foundation of an independently identifiable target. A Level-3 account therefore remains sufficient.

**Material 3b/3c disagreement:** The minority evaluator accepts the importance of Bloom's return, the Bloom–Stephen conjunction, and Molly's affirmation, but argues that these remain partial or local: Stephen leaves, Bloom and Molly remain irreducibly separate, and incompatible representational systems are not globally synthesized. The majority interprets those same unresolved elements differently: their persistence is not evidence against 3c because integration under the protocol requires contradiction to be contained rather than abolished. This is the decisive interpretive fork in the seven-report dataset.

Remote elements repeatedly change one another's significance rather than functioning as merely accumulated components. The main limiting argument is that significant episode-level structures retain some decomposability, preventing movement toward maximal interdependence.

**Material disagreement:** Minimal. The total spread is only 0.4.

**AD — Abstraction Depth: 9.0**
**Evaluator range:** 8.8–9.1

The evaluators consistently identify deep movement from concrete embodied and urban experience through perception, memory, language, cultural and mythic structures, narrative conventions, and finally meta-representation in which representational systems themselves become represented. The work preserves relations back to concrete particulars rather than abandoning them at higher levels of abstraction.

The minor disagreement concerns whether the artifact fully establishes a sufficiently invariant global meta-architecture for the 9 anchor, as opposed to remaining deliberately plural and locally autonomous.

**CD — Constraint Density: 8.7**
**Evaluator range:** 8.4–9.1

The common evidence is the simultaneous coordination of chronology, geography, character continuity, recurring motifs, linguistic differentiation, episode-specific formal rules, thematic structures, bodily realism, intertextual correspondences, and long-range echoes. These requirements interact, so changes in one region can propagate into others.

The disagreement is about solution-space compression. Higher scorers judge the heterogeneous constraints sufficiently coupled to leave an unusually narrow successful construction class; lower scorers emphasize that many local passages, correspondences, stylistic choices, and episode-level realizations could change without destroying the artifact's central operation.

**ID — Inferential Distance: 8.6**
**Evaluator range:** 8.2–8.8

All evaluators place the artifact well above routine restructuring. They repeatedly cite the non-obvious construction that converts an ordinary urban day into interacting psychological, linguistic, mythic, bodily, cultural, formal, and recursively representational systems while maintaining continuity across them.

The variation comes mainly from evidentiary caution about reconstructing the original creative problem-space. Lower scorers argue that an artifact alone gives weaker evidence for the degree of underdetermination facing its construction than a proof or documented reasoning trace would.

**IC — Integrative Compression: 8.6**
**Evaluator range:** 8.2–8.7

The repeated compression argument centers on a comparatively compact architecture—one day, one city, a small central consciousness/relationship network, recurrent motifs, embodiment, correspondence, and formal variation—organizing an enormous heterogeneous field without erasing local distinctions.

All seven also identify the same ceiling: integration is not identical to compression. Much of the artifact's complexity remains explicitly elaborated rather than economically reconstructible from a remarkably small governing architecture. The disagreement is therefore one of degree, not structure.

**GR — Generalization Reach: 7.3**
**Evaluator range:** 6.5–7.7

This is both the lowest consensus dimension and the least stable one. The shared positive case is that the artifact's architecture transfers across substantially varied consciousnesses, social situations, experiential states, registers, memory structures, embodied conditions, and representational modes.

The shared limitation is equally consistent: broad applicability to human experience does not automatically demonstrate structural transfer across markedly heterogeneous external problem-spaces. Evaluators differ over how much transfer within the artifact and across human/social/linguistic cases is sufficient to reach or exceed the 7 anchor. This produces the largest dimensional range, 1.2 points.

# Consensus Evaluation

*Ulysses* receives a consensus classification of **3c — Integrated Recursive Consciousness**, with a **median CMS of 860** and a mean CMS of **851.7**.

The seven evaluations converge first on the novel's recursive operation. Its formal transformations are not treated merely as stylistic complexity or elaborate symbolism. Across the reports, perception, memory, language, embodiment, cultural inheritance, narrative convention, and perspective repeatedly become part of what the artifact itself investigates. Experienced reality is continually reconstructed through different forms of consciousness and representation. On that evidence, ordinary symbolic organization is insufficient to describe the completed operation, placing the work securely within Level 3.

The principal classification question is therefore not whether *Ulysses* reaches recursive consciousness, but whether its recursion remains fractured at 3b or becomes integrated at 3c. Six of seven evaluators conclude that the completed novel crosses that boundary. Their case does not depend on treating the ending as simple reconciliation. Mortality, estrangement, infidelity, epistemic uncertainty, imperfect communication, unstable self-understanding, and incompatible perspectives remain operative. What changes is that these contradictions become inhabitable within a larger architecture of embodied continuity, memory, relation, recurrence, domestic return, plurality, and affirmation. In the majority reading, the work integrates fracture without abolishing it—the defining 3c operation.

The minority 3b evaluation identifies the genuine pressure point in that conclusion. It argues that Bloom and Stephen's conjunction remains temporary, that Bloom and Molly remain separate consciousnesses, and that Molly's final affirmation never becomes a sufficiently global governing structure for the novel's unresolved epistemological and existential fractures. On this reading, *Ulysses* contains moments of integration but ultimately inhabits fracture more decisively than it transcends it. This objection is material, but it does not overturn the six-evaluator majority that understands unresolved plurality itself as something the completed architecture successfully contains.

The upper boundary is considerably clearer. None of the seven evaluators finds G warranted. The reports consistently distinguish the novel's extraordinary internal generativity from the protocol's stronger requirement of explanatory relocation. Its stylistic and perspectival systems radically transform how consciousness and experience can be represented, but the evaluations do not find that they establish a new generative mechanism that replaces what explains an independently identifiable target. Level 3 therefore remains sufficient.

The dimensional profile is similarly coherent. **Relational Load (9.2)** is the clearest strength: the artifact sustains unusually dense consequential relations across psychological, linguistic, temporal, spatial, bodily, social, mythic, cultural, and formal structures. **Abstraction Depth (9.0)** is nearly as stable, reflecting its sustained movement from concrete experience into meta-representational structures while retaining the underlying relationships. **Constraint Density (8.7)**, **Inferential Distance (8.6)**, and **Integrative Compression (8.6)** remain strongly elevated, though evaluators differ over how narrowly the viable construction space is constrained, how confidently the originating inferential leap can be reconstructed, and how much of the artifact's enormous integration is genuinely compressed rather than explicitly elaborated.

**Generalization Reach (7.3)** is the consistent relative bottleneck and the largest source of numerical disagreement. All seven reports recognize substantial transfer across consciousnesses, social situations, registers, embodied states, and modes of representation. They also agree that broad relevance to human experience cannot simply be converted into evidence of cross-domain structural transfer. The 6.5–7.7 spread reflects different judgments about exactly where that transfer firewall should bind, not disagreement about the underlying architecture.

The resulting consensus is therefore a high-magnitude but structurally differentiated **3c**: an artifact distinguished most strongly by exceptional relational coordination and deep recursive abstraction, whose enormous formal and semantic generativity remains below G because it reorganizes and investigates experience without relocating its explanatory foundation.

**Final consensus: 3c | 860.**
