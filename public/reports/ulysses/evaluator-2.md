## Operational Order (TORC): **Level 3b — Fractured Recursive Consciousness**

*Ulysses* is best classified as **3b**, not because of its difficulty, stylistic virtuosity, symbolism, formal experimentation, or canonical status, but because its dominant operation recursively investigates how consciousness constructs and encounters reality while refusing to consolidate that investigation into a single coherent existential orientation.

The protocol requires the lowest classification sufficient to explain the completed artifact and explicitly warns against treating complexity, metafiction, nonlinear form, or psychological sophistication as evidence of higher order by themselves.  

### Why Level 2 is insufficient

Symbolic organization is undeniably pervasive: recurrent motifs, mythic correspondences, bodily imagery, religious structures, linguistic echoes, and the Homeric scaffolding continuously transform represented events into larger patterns. But **symbolism alone does not adequately explain what the novel is doing**.

Across the completed artifact, representation itself repeatedly becomes unstable and exposed as consciousness-dependent. The same city and ordinary day are reconstructed through radically different perceptual, linguistic, mnemonic, rhetorical, and epistemic organizations. “Proteus” makes perception and cognition themselves the problem; “Sirens” reorganizes experience through acoustic pattern; “Cyclops” pits narrative registers against each other; “Oxen of the Sun” makes historical styles of language constitutive of what can be represented; “Circe” destabilizes distinctions among desire, hallucination, memory, theatrical representation, and event; “Ithaca” subjects intimate human experience to an artificially exhaustive interrogative system; “Penelope” finally removes much of the mediating narrative apparatus and gives another consciousness its own organizing field.

Thus the formal variations are not detachable ornament. They repeatedly demonstrate that represented reality cannot be separated cleanly from the forms of consciousness and language through which it becomes intelligible. That is precisely the protocol's threshold for recursive consciousness: representation, interpretation, orientation, or their conditions become objects of inquiry, and symbolic organization ceases to be sufficient. 

### Why 3b rather than 3a

The central fracture is not principally something wrong with Dublin that could be corrected by better institutions, politics, morality, or social arrangements. Those fractures certainly occur—nationalism, colonial relations, religion, masculinity, antisemitism, economic precarity—but they are embedded within a more fundamental problem.

The novel persistently confronts the inability of consciousness to obtain an unmediated, completely stable relation to existence: perception is perspectival, memory intrudes upon the present, language both reveals and distorts, identity changes according to representational context, death cannot be cognitively mastered, and separate consciousnesses never become perfectly mutually transparent.

That fracture survives removal of the novel's conspicuous stylistic distortions. Stephen's relation to mortality, guilt, embodiment and epistemic uncertainty; Bloom's relation to grief, sexuality, estrangement and other minds; Molly's irreducibly separate consciousness—all remain existential problems rather than artifacts merely produced by experimental prose. This meets the protocol's Existential Resistance requirement for 3b. 

### Why it does not reach 3c

The strongest upward case is the completed movement toward Bloom's return, Bloom and Stephen's temporary conjunction, and finally Molly's affirmative “yes.” But the **Integration Resistance Principle** prevents treating reconciliation, hope, emotional resolution, or closure as 3c automatically. 3c requires the preserved existential contradiction to become integrated into a coherent existential orientation. 

That additional operation is not sufficiently established.

Bloom and Stephen meet but do not form a stable synthesis. Stephen leaves. Bloom and Molly remain separate consciousnesses. Molly's concluding affirmation accommodates enormous contradiction—desire, resentment, memory, sexuality, affection—but does not become a governing architecture capable of integrating the novel's epistemological and existential fractures globally. The plurality of incompatible representational systems also remains deliberately unreconciled.

The completed artifact therefore **inhabits and exposes the fracture more decisively than it integrates it**. Calling the ending 3c would depend too heavily upon turning local affirmation and partial reconciliation into a global existential synthesis.

Hence **3b is the lowest sufficient classification**.

### Why it does not reach G

The high formal complexity makes the G firewall particularly important. Under the protocol, G cannot be inferred from extraordinary complexity, abstraction, novelty, recursion, or artifact-internal generativity. A genuine G operation must relocate the explanatory foundation of an **independently identifiable target**, with the disclosed structure generating explanatory consequences for that target rather than merely organizing the artifact's own representational world. 

Applied explicitly:

**Independent Target Test:** plausible targets include consciousness, ordinary human experience, linguistic mediation, or the relation between consciousness and reality. These are independently identifiable, so the artifact passes this preliminary requirement.

**Generative-mechanism test:** the novel demonstrates numerous ways language, memory, sensory organization, genre, and perspective mediate experience. But it does not establish one newly disclosed mechanism that explains *why* independently existing phenomena across the target domain take their forms.

**Explanatory Replacement Principle:** the recursive account remains sufficient. We do not need to posit a relocated explanatory foundation beyond the novel's investigation of consciousness/representation to explain the completed artifact.

**Explanatory-Object Firewall:** transforming the means by which experience is represented is not itself equivalent to changing what explains consciousness or experience.

**Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall:** perhaps the most decisive failure. *Ulysses* generates astonishing semantic consequences by changing styles, perspectives, linguistic rules, correspondences, and representational systems, but the protocol expressly excludes artifact-internal generation from G unless it relocates the explanation of an independent target.

Accordingly, **G is not warranted**. Since G fails, GΩ and M cannot become the classification route. The protocol itself stresses that G requires explanatory relocation rather than accumulated lower-order complexity.
