## 1. Operational Order (TORC)

**Classification: Level 3c — Integrated Recursive Consciousness**

*Ulysses*, treated strictly as a completed artifact rather than through its canonical reputation, is best classified at **3c**. Its dominant global operation is not adequately described as symbolic organization alone. Across its shifting styles, interior monologues, linguistic transformations, mythic correspondences, competing perspectives, and recurrent motifs, representation itself—how consciousness converts experience into an intelligible world—becomes part of what the novel investigates.

The protocol makes the decisive Level-3 transition occur when symbolic organization becomes insufficient and “the relationship between consciousness and reality itself” becomes the dominant object of investigation.  That criterion fits the artifact unusually well. Dublin is not merely represented and symbolized: the same world is continually reconstructed through different consciousnesses, discourses, memories, fantasies, linguistic registers, cultural schemas, and representational conventions.

The completed movement nevertheless goes beyond **3b fractured recursion**. The artifact repeatedly exposes instability—between perception and actuality, language and referent, self and social identity, memory and present experience, embodiment and abstraction—but does not terminate in that fracture. Its final large-scale movement reincorporates contradiction into continued embodied, domestic, relational consciousness. The contradiction remains rather than being theoretically solved. That is precisely the protocol's distinction between 3b confrontation and 3c integration: 3c must preserve the contradiction while producing a coherent existential orientation rather than mere closure or consolation.  

### Boundary analysis

**Why 3c rather than 3b.** The novel certainly satisfies the underlying 3b structure: consciousness repeatedly encounters a reality that cannot be stabilized by a single explanatory perspective. Stephen's intellectual alienation, Bloom's social and personal dislocations, competing accounts of identity and history, the mutability of language, and the radical perspectival transformations of ordinary events make the fracture structural rather than simply a represented theme. The protocol requires 3b's existential contradiction to survive removal of stylistic and symbolic distortion; here it does—the underlying problems of mortality, embodiment, fidelity, loneliness, identity, memory, belonging, and the imperfect accessibility of other minds remain. 

But **3b is insufficient** because confrontation with fracture does not explain the completed operation. The work progressively incorporates mutually incompatible modes rather than selecting one as authoritative. Bloom's return, the partial Bloom/Stephen conjunction and separation, and especially Molly's concluding consciousness do not abolish contradiction. They relocate it inside an ongoing, embodied affirmation of existence. That satisfies the 3c requirement that “the contradiction remains operative” while consciousness develops an orientation capable of containing it. 

**Why not G.** This is the crucial ceiling. The protocol expressly forbids inferring G from exceptional complexity, abstraction, recursion, ingenuity, or formal innovation. G requires **explanatory relocation**: a previously sufficient explanatory basis for an independently identifiable target must become inadequate because the artifact supplies a generative explanatory mechanism. 

Applying the required G gates:

- **Independent target:** Plausible targets can be stated—ordinary human consciousness, lived experience, linguistic mediation, or the construction of subjective reality. 
- **Previously unseen generative structure:** Not sufficiently established. The novel constructs extraordinarily powerful mechanisms for rendering consciousness and representation, but its plurality of techniques does not compel one newly disclosed generative mechanism explaining why independently existing phenomena take the forms they do. 
- **Generative mechanism vs. explanatory schema:** The work reveals and enacts relations among language, memory, embodiment, culture, perception, and identity, but largely through recursive representation and integration. It does not establish a single structure that repeatedly generates nontrivial explanatory consequences across independently constrained target instances. 
- **Explanatory replacement:** Not satisfied. A 3c account remains sufficient to explain the completed artifact: recursive exposure of consciousness/reality fractures followed by integration without elimination. 
- **Explanatory-object firewall:** Its transformation of narrative method and representation is extraordinary, but the protocol explicitly says transformation in representation, method, or compression does not itself constitute G. 
- **Artifact-internal generativity firewall:** The novel generates an immense semantic and representational world through its stylistic systems, but artifact-internal generation is explicitly insufficient unless it relocates explanation for an independently identifiable target. 

The protocol is categorical here: artifact-internal semantic/formal generativity remains below G unless an independent target's explanatory foundation is relocated.  Thus **3c is the lowest sufficient classification**.
