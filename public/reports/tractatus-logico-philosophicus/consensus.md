## 4. Consensus analysis

Across the seven reports, the recurring G-level claim is stable: the artifact identifies meaningful propositional representation, its relation to reality, logical necessity, and the limits or failures of philosophical discourse as an independently identifiable target, and treats the picture/logical-form architecture as explanatory.

The recurrent chain is approximately: facts/world → picturing → shared logical form → propositions and truth conditions → truth-functional/logical structure → limits of meaningful saying → philosophical clarification.

The closest boundary is G rather than 3c. Recursive and self-applicative material is present, but a 3c description leaves the dominant explanatory architecture underdescribed. GΩ is not established because successor-baseline necessity requires independent historical inquiry, and M is not established because framework-space necessity across alternative adequate frameworks is not demonstrated.

# Consensus Evaluation

Seven independent TORC/DIP evaluations converge unanimously on **G — Generative Consciousness / Explanatory Relocation**, with a headline consensus **CMS of 868**. The mean CMS is **865.4**. Despite independent scoring, all seven evaluators reached the same Operational Order and all reported Moderate confidence, making the categorical result unusually stable.

The common basis for G is the artifact's treatment of meaningful representation as an independently identifiable explanatory target. Across the reports, the central architecture is consistently reconstructed through the relations among facts, pictures, logical form, propositions, truth-functional structure, logic, and the limits of meaningful saying. On the evaluators' shared reading, these elements do not merely form an abstract philosophical system. They alter what is taken to explain how propositions can represent reality and why logic and philosophical discourse have the forms assigned to them.

The decisive boundary remains the strict 3c → G generative-mechanism gate. Every evaluator identifies some version of the same vulnerability: several of the artifact's deepest transitions are architectonic, compressed, or asserted rather than exhaustively reconstructed. The unanimous judgment is nevertheless that the picture/logical-form structure generates enough nontrivial consequences for the independent target to qualify as explanatory relocation rather than merely an exceptionally deep explanatory schema.
