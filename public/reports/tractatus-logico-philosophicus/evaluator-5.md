## 1. Operational Order (TORC)

# **G — Generative Consciousness / Explanatory Relocation**

The highest warranted classification is **G**.

The *Tractatus* does more than symbolically organize philosophical material or recursively examine the relation between consciousness, language, and reality. Its dominant operation is to replace a familiar explanatory picture of philosophical problems—one in which philosophical propositions state truths or falsehoods about special metaphysical objects—with a structural account in which **propositions picture possible facts by sharing logical form with reality, logic supplies the limits of representation, and many traditional philosophical problems arise from attempts to say what can only be shown**. The protocol defines G precisely as a change in the explanatory foundation by which an independently identifiable target becomes intelligible, rather than an increase in complexity or another interpretation. 

The artifact explicitly establishes this explanatory architecture. Proposition 4.01 identifies a proposition as a picture/model of reality; 4.12–4.1212 argues that logical form cannot itself be represented but is exhibited by meaningful representation; 4.112 recasts philosophy as logical clarification rather than doctrine; and 6.53–6.54 culminates in a method under which metaphysical pseudo-propositions are dissolved and even the book's own elucidatory propositions are ultimately discarded. 

The important point for TORC is not whether this theory is ultimately correct. The protocol expressly permits a fallible or subsequently rejected framework to qualify for G if it nevertheless performs genuine explanatory relocation rather than merely redescribing material in its own vocabulary. 

### Boundary analysis: G versus 3c

**Why G rather than 3c:** Level 3c would be sufficient if the dominant operation were principally the integration of an existential contradiction into a coherent orientation while preserving the contradiction. The *Tractatus* contains something resembling this near its end—the limits of language, the unsayable, the problem of life, the mystical, and the ladder—but that is not enough to explain the whole work. Its earlier ontology of facts, picture theory, truth-function account, analysis of logical form, treatment of inference, distinction between science and philosophy, and account of philosophical nonsense are not merely components of an existential reconciliation. They function as a unified explanatory architecture for **why meaningful representation is possible and why certain apparent philosophical questions fail**. The protocol requires advancement beyond Level 3 where recursive consciousness no longer adequately explains the dominant operation. 

In particular, the book claims that philosophy is an activity of logical clarification, not a competing body of theoretical propositions, and explains philosophical pseudo-problems through misunderstanding the logic of language.  Thus, a 3c interpretation captures an important terminal movement but leaves substantial portions of the completed artifact operationally unexplained.

### Explicit G gate analysis

**1. Independent Target Test — PASS.**

The target can be specified independently of the artifact: **meaningful propositional representation and the resulting status of philosophical discourse**. This is not merely “the symbolic world generated by the *Tractatus*.” The target exists independently: how propositions represent states of affairs, what makes propositions meaningful, what logical consequence consists in, and what happens when philosophical language exceeds those representational conditions. This satisfies the protocol's requirement that the explanatory target not simply be the artifact's own construction. 

**2. Previously unseen generative structure — PASS.**

Within the completed artifact, the operative structure is approximately:

**world as structured facts ↔ possible configuration of objects ↔ thought/proposition as structurally articulated picture ↔ shared logical form ↔ truth-functional articulation ↔ limits on meaningful saying ↔ philosophical clarification**

Individual propositions are not independent theses stapled together. Later claims about logic, philosophy, the subject, and the sayable depend upon earlier relations between fact, picture, proposition, and logical form. Proposition 4.01's picture relation and 4.12–4.1212's distinction between representable content and exhibited logical form are especially central. 

**3. Generative Mechanism / Explanatory Schema Test — PASS, with some residual vulnerability.**

This is more than a cosmological or conceptual “picture.” The common structural relation between proposition and possible fact generates consequences: why propositions can be true or false; why logical propositions behave differently from factual propositions; why inference can be read from truth-conditions; why logical form cannot itself be represented as another fact; why philosophy cannot operate as another natural science; and why certain attempted propositions collapse into nonsense. The protocol requires precisely such nontrivial explanatory consequences across instances of an independently identifiable target. 

The vulnerability is that several crucial transitions are asserted with greater confidence than argumentative derivation. That limits inferential scoring below, but does not reduce the entire structure to mere stipulation: the architecture repeatedly constrains what follows elsewhere in the book.

**4. Explanatory Replacement Principle — PASS.**

The proposed account does not simply add one interpretation of metaphysics. If successful on its own terms, it removes the previous model under which philosophy straightforwardly makes substantive propositions about metaphysical entities alongside empirical discourse. Philosophy instead becomes clarification of the conditions of meaningful representation. The previous explanatory basis therefore cannot remain simultaneously sufficient. That is the protocol's required replacement rather than addition. 

**5. Explanatory-Object Firewall — PASS.**

The innovation is not merely a superior notation, algorithm, calculus, or representation technique. The book certainly uses formal devices, but its explanatory target is what makes propositions meaningful and logical at all. Formalization serves the explanatory claim rather than constituting the whole achievement. That distinguishes explanatory relocation from improved manipulation of an unchanged object. 

**6. Computational/Procedural Generativity Firewall — PASS.**

Truth operations and formal transformations are present, but G does not rest on their procedural productivity. The generative claim concerns the structural relation among proposition, possibility, logical form, and world. The artifact therefore does not qualify merely because it can procedurally generate propositions. 

**7. Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall — PASS.**

The explanatory structure is used to account for independently recognizable phenomena—representation, inference, logical necessity, philosophical pseudo-problems—not merely to generate the semantics of the book itself. 

**8. Fallible Explanation Principle — PASS.**

Its explanatory success need not establish the ultimate truth of logical atomism or the picture theory. What matters is that independently constraining phenomena—truth/falsity, propositional articulation, inference, contradiction, ordinary language, and philosophical discourse—are organized so that the framework has nontrivial consequences rather than merely defining itself true. 

**9. G necessary conditions — PASS.**

The dominant operation discloses a generative structure; relocates the explanatory foundation; is not fully captured by Levels 1–3; and uses that relocated basis to organize its target domain. These are the protocol's four necessary G conditions. 

### Why not GΩ?

**GΩ is not warranted in this independent artifact-only evaluation.**

The protocol imposes a distinct evidential condition on GΩ: successor-baseline necessity must be **independently demonstrated through actual successor inquiry or practice in the native domain**. Fame, influence, citation, controversy, or canonical status do not establish it. 

That evidence is deliberately outside the present artifact-first judgment. Nothing internal to the completed *Tractatus* can itself demonstrate how adequate later inquiry actually proceeded. Assigning GΩ from the work's famous place in philosophy would directly violate both the independent-evaluation instruction and the protocol's independent successor-evidence requirement. Therefore the warranted classification terminates at **G**.

M is consequently not reached or inferred merely from the book's extensive discussion of limits, possibility, logic, and what can or cannot be said: the protocol explicitly warns that theorizing conditions or limits is not itself proof of an unavoidable constraint across the entire space of adequate alternative frameworks.

## 2. Cognitive Magnitude Score

The scores below were fixed dimension-by-dimension before computing CMS, as required by the protocol. Decimal positions interpolate between substantive integer anchors; CMS is then the equal-weight mean multiplied by 100 and rounded to the nearest integer.  

### **RL — Relational Load: 8.8**

**Principal evidence:** the artifact must coordinate ontology, objects/facts, picturing, thought, proposition, logical form, truth conditions, truth-functions, inference, logic, natural science, philosophy, the subject/world boundary, value, and the say/show distinction. Crucially, many are nonlocal dependencies: conclusions around 6.5–6.54 depend upon distinctions erected in propositions 1–5 rather than operating as an independent concluding essay. Propositions 5.6–5.61, for example, connect language, logic, and the limits of the world. 

The complete 8 anchor requires dense cross-structural dependency in which remote relations constrain one another. That is clearly met. The artifact approaches 9 because these relations span ontology, semantics, logic, epistemic representation, philosophy, and value. 

**Why not 9.0:** the heterogeneity is substantial, but not every major domain is equally reciprocally coupled. The ethical/mystical terminus, for example, depends on the limit architecture more strongly than the earlier logical machinery depends on it. Hence exceptional heterogeneous *mutual* interdependence is approached but not fully established.

### **AD — Abstraction Depth: 9.2**

**Principal evidence:** the work does not merely abstract from objects to propositions. It moves through facts, possible facts, pictures, logical form, truth-conditions, general propositional form, the conditions of representability, the limits of representational language, and finally the status of its own propositions as elucidatory objects to be transcended. Proposition 4.12 asks what representation must share with reality while simultaneously arguing that this shared form cannot itself be represented; 6.54 then turns the framework reflexively upon its own sentences. 

The protocol's 9 anchor requires very deep invariant/meta-level structures organizing broad or heterogeneous material with disciplined relation preservation. That condition is satisfied. 

Movement beyond 9 comes from the additional self-application of the distinction between meaningful saying and what cannot itself be said. I do not place it near 10 because the highest abstraction occasionally outruns explicit structural derivation; universality and philosophical altitude alone cannot substitute for constrained correspondence under the protocol.

### **CD — Constraint Density: 8.6**

**Principal evidence:** the system tightly couples multiple requirements. A meaningful proposition must possess articulated structure, admit bipolarity, relate to possible reality, obey logical form, participate in truth-functional structure, and avoid treating formal relations as ordinary factual relations. Adjusting one claim often propagates elsewhere—for example, permitting logical form itself to be ordinarily stated would disrupt the say/show distinction, the characterization of logic, and the proposed philosophical method. 

The complete 8 anchor—heterogeneous constraints interacting across levels so that local optimization compromises other requirements—is satisfied. 

The artifact moves substantially toward 9 because ontology, semantics, logic, and philosophical method converge on a narrow architecture. But 9 requires **exceptional solution-space compression** under numerous strongly coupled heterogeneous constraints. Some local doctrines—especially parts of the ontology, psychology, mathematics, ethics, and mysticism—could be modified without destroying every central explanatory achievement. Thus 9 is not fully established.

### **ID — Inferential Distance: 8.5**

**Principal evidence:** the artifact performs several very nonroutine restructurings: treating propositions as pictures rather than mere names or complexes; deriving logical consequence from truth-ground relations; construing logical truths as structurally distinctive rather than factual; turning philosophical problems into failures in the logic of language; and finally applying the say/show distinction reflexively to the artifact's own propositions. 

This clearly satisfies the protocol's 8 anchor: highly non-obvious restructuring with meaningful alternatives and no routine route. 

It moves toward 9 because several central transitions radically reorganize an underdetermined philosophical problem-space. I stop at **8.5**, however, because the *Tractatus* frequently states indispensable relations aphoristically rather than reconstructing them with enough evidentiary support to satisfy the complete 9 criterion of exceptional inferential reconstruction. Difficulty, surprise, and boldness do not substitute for demonstrated inference under the protocol.

### **IC — Integrative Compression: 9.2**

**Principal evidence:** an unusually compact architecture—fact, object, picture, logical form, proposition, truth-function, showing/saying—coordinates a very large heterogeneous field: ontology, semantics, logic, inference, philosophy, natural science, subjectivity, value, and the boundaries of expression. Its hierarchical decimal structure itself permits later propositions to unpack consequences from earlier organizing claims rather than separately rebuilding each topic.

The protocol's 9 anchor requires very large heterogeneous complexity to be reorganized through remarkably economical architecture while preserving unusually fine structure and enabling extensive reconstruction or derivation.  That is satisfied here; the score is not awarded merely because the book is short, since the protocol expressly distinguishes brevity from compression. 

Movement beyond 9 is warranted by the striking economy with which the same few structural distinctions recur across otherwise distant questions. The limitation is that compression is not uniformly reconstructive: some terminal claims concerning value, the mystical, and the transcendence of the book's propositions are not derivable with the same fine-grained necessity as the logical-semantic core.

### **GR — Generalization Reach: 8.3**

**Principal evidence:** the core representation architecture transfers from elementary propositions to complex propositions, logical implication, ordinary language, propositions concerning belief, the relation between logic and science, and the diagnosis of philosophical discourse. The same distinction between factual saying and exhibited formal structure is reapplied at multiple levels rather than being rebuilt ad hoc. 

The protocol's 8 anchor requires substantial power across markedly heterogeneous cases with limited reconstruction and strong robustness. That is satisfied. 

I do not award 9. The apparent universality of “the limits of language,” logic, and world cannot itself count as demonstrated transfer; the protocol expressly forbids equating claimed universality with GR and requires actual structural transfer across distant problem-spaces.  Transfer is powerful across representational/logical/philosophical problems but becomes more contentious and less structurally preserved in ethics, aesthetics, value, and the mystical.

### Committed dimensional architecture

**RL / AD / CD / ID / IC / GR: 8.8 / 9.2 / 8.6 / 8.5 / 9.2 / 8.3**

Inferential distance is somewhat lower because several decisive structural moves are asserted more compactly than they are demonstrated. Generalization reach is the lowest dimension—not because the work lacks universal ambition, but because the protocol requires demonstrated structural transfer rather than broad applicability or universal claims.
