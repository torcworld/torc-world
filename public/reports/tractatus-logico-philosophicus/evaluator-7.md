## 1. Operational Order (TORC)

# **G — Generative Consciousness: Explanatory Relocation**

The *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus* warrants **G**, rather than Level 3, because its dominant operation is not merely to reflect recursively on consciousness, representation, or philosophical contradiction. It proposes a compact structural account—facts in logical space, picturing, logical form, propositional sense, truth-functional construction, and the saying/showing distinction—and uses that architecture to **change what explains a substantial class of philosophical problems**.

The protocol reserves G for cases in which a previously sufficient explanatory basis becomes inadequate and a new structure explains why independently identifiable target phenomena take the forms they do; complexity or universality alone is insufficient.  

The independently identifiable target is: **the possibility and limits of meaningful representation, together with the recurrent production of philosophical pseudo-problems concerning logic, world, subject, value, and metaphysics**.

The artifact relocates the explanatory basis of that target. It begins by construing the world structurally as facts rather than merely things.  It then identifies propositions as models or pictures whose capacity to represent reality depends on shared logical form.  From this, it derives a distinction between what propositions can state and what their structure can only show. 

That structure is then used globally: philosophical confusion is diagnosed as misunderstanding the logic of language; the general form of proposition is treated as the essence of description; the limits of language delimit the world available to representation; logic is not another empirical theory but something reflected in the structure of representation itself.  The concluding propositions make the method reflexive: the work's own philosophical sentences are finally to be surpassed once their elucidatory function has been performed. 

### G gate analysis

**Independent Target Test — PASS.** The target can be specified without making the *Tractatus* itself the object: meaningful linguistic representation and the generation of philosophical pseudo-problems. The protocol explicitly requires such an independently identifiable target. 

**Generative mechanism vs. explanatory schema — PASS.** The picture/logical-form architecture does more than arrange language, reality, logic, and philosophy into a novel picture. It generates explanatory consequences: why propositions can represent facts, why logical propositions function differently from empirical propositions, why logical form cannot itself be represented in the same way, why certain metaphysical constructions fail to achieve propositional sense, and why philosophical clarification differs from ordinary factual assertion. The G criterion specifically distinguishes this consequence-generating role from an encompassing but merely stipulated ontology. 

**Explanatory-Object Firewall — PASS.** The achievement is not merely a new symbolism or computational technique. The account claims to change **what explains meaningful representation and philosophical confusion**—logical picturing and conditions of sense—rather than merely how already-defined propositions are manipulated. 

**Computational/Procedural Generativity Firewall — PASS.** Truth-functional operations and notation are present, but G does not rest on their procedural novelty. Their role is embedded in a broader explanation of propositional possibility and logical form. 

**Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall — PASS.** The structure does not merely explain how the book generates its own semantic organization. Its claims concern an independently existing target—language, propositionhood, inference, representation, logic, and philosophical discourse. 

**Fallible Explanation Principle — PASS.** G does not require ultimate truth. What matters is whether the framework produces nontrivial explanatory consequences concerning independently constraining phenomena rather than merely redescribing everything in definitions of its own making.  The *Tractatus* does so by tying its framework to identifiable structural features of propositions, inference, contradiction, tautology, representation, and ordinary language.

**Explanatory Replacement Principle — PASS.** The book explicitly reframes philosophical problems: rather than treating many of them as substantive questions requiring metaphysical answers, it explains their apparent problem-status through failures to understand linguistic/logical conditions. Its preface announces precisely this methodological displacement, and proposition 4.003 attributes many philosophical questions to misunderstanding the logic of language.  A merely recursive reading therefore leaves a central global operation unexplained.

### Boundary analysis

**Why G rather than 3c.** Level 3c would require the central achievement to be integration of an existential contradiction into a coherent orientation. The protocol defines 3c specifically in terms of preserving existential fracture while integrating it rather than resolving it.  The *Tractatus* certainly contains recursive material concerning subject, limits, ethics, value, and the unsayable. Its treatment of the subject as a limit rather than an object is strongly recursive.  But those operations are subordinate to a more encompassing explanatory structure: **why representation has limits and why philosophical propositions cross them**. Removing the picture/logical-form/sense architecture would destroy the functional basis of the later reflections; retaining only existential integration would not explain the work as a whole.

Thus Levels 1–3 are insufficient in the protocol's specific sense: recursion explains important consequences of the system but not the structure that generates those consequences.

**Why not GΩ.** GΩ requires something additional that G does not: independent evidence that the relocation becomes an **unavoidable baseline constraint on adequate successor inquiry**. The protocol explicitly forbids substituting fame, citation, influence, canonization, or later discussion for this test.  Successor frameworks must be unable to conduct adequate inquiry while simply ignoring the transformed explanatory problem. 

That stronger condition is **not established by the artifact itself**, and this independent evaluation provides no admissible successor-practice evidence demonstrating unavoidable necessity. Its canonical or influential status cannot fill that gap. Under the protocol's burden-of-proof rule, the classification therefore stops at **G**.

M is consequently not reached either. More fundamentally, the *Tractatus* advances a strong account of the conditions and limits of representation, but the protocol warns explicitly that theorizing constitutive conditions is not enough for M: the artifact would need to establish a constraint that reappears across alternative adequate frameworks and cannot be escaped by replacing its own framework.  That universal framework-space necessity is not demonstrated.

### **RL — Relational Load: 8.5**

**Bounding anchors:** 8 satisfied; 9 not fully satisfied.

The protocol's 8 anchor requires dense cross-structural dependency, while 9 requires exceptional heterogeneous interdependence spanning substantially different levels or domains. 

The work requires simultaneous coordination of world/fact/object, picture/reality, thought/proposition, logical form, sense/reference, truth-function, inference, logic, language, subject, ethics, and saying/showing. Importantly, these are not independent chapters: conclusions about the limits of language depend on the earlier account of representation; the account of logic constrains propositionhood; the treatment of subject and value follows from the boundaries imposed by the representational system. 

Movement toward 9 is substantial because logical, linguistic, ontological, epistemic, and ethical levels interact. I stop below 9 because the governing relational field is extraordinarily coupled but intentionally narrow and architecturally unified rather than maximally heterogeneous.

**Principal evidence:** propositions 1–5.6 and 6 jointly require the same representational architecture to govern ontology, logic, language, subjectivity, and philosophical limits.

### **AD — Abstraction Depth: 9.0**

**Bounding anchor:** 9 fully satisfied.

The 9 anchor requires very deep invariant/meta-level structures organizing broad or heterogeneous material with unusually disciplined relation preservation. 

The artifact moves through several levels: facts → pictures → thoughts → propositions → logical form → general propositional form → conditions of describability → limits of language/world → distinction between stating and showing → reflexive status of philosophical elucidation. It does not merely make universal claims; it repeatedly asks what must remain invariant for representation itself to be possible. Propositions cannot represent the logical form they share with reality; that form is exhibited through their functioning.  The general form of proposition is then connected to the essence of description itself. 

This clearly crosses the complete 9 threshold. I do not interpolate farther toward 10 because the submitted protocol evidence available here does not warrant treating “maximum abstraction” as a generic reward for philosophical depth.

**Principal evidence:** the movement from particular propositions to conditions of representation, then to meta-limits on representing those conditions.

### **CD — Constraint Density: 8.3**

**Bounding anchors:** 8 satisfied; 9 not satisfied.

The 8 anchor requires heterogeneous requirements interacting across levels such that local optimization regularly compromises other requirements; 9 requires those constraints to leave a very narrow class of successful constructions. 

A successful construction must simultaneously preserve correspondence between fact and proposition; separate names, elementary propositions, complexes, and truth-functions; maintain the internal role of logical form; avoid treating logical constants as ordinary objects; explain inference without converting logic into empirical theory; accommodate ordinary language; delimit philosophical discourse; and finally account reflexively for the work's own apparently philosophical propositions.

These constraints visibly interact. For example, allowing logical form to be straightforwardly stated would undermine the saying/showing distinction; making logic a factual theory would undermine its transcendental role; treating the metaphysical subject as an item within the world would conflict with its boundary function.

The work exceeds ordinary dense constraint coordination, but 9 is not fully established because significant individual theses can be modified without obviously destroying every central achievement. The protocol explicitly warns against confusing complexity with genuinely narrow construction space. 

**Principal evidence:** tight dependencies among picture theory, logical form, truth-functional analysis, logical necessity, and the limit on meaningful philosophical assertion.

### **ID — Inferential Distance: 8.8**

**Bounding anchors:** 8 satisfied; very strong approach to 9.

The protocol defines 8 as a highly non-obvious restructuring with meaningful alternatives and no routine route; 9 requires a result deeply underdetermined even for a strong domain reasoner. 

The key inferential movement is substantial: from structural features of propositions and representation to a systematic distinction between meaningful factual propositions, tautological logic, and pseudo-propositional attempts to state what can only be shown; from there to a transformed conception of philosophy itself. The leap from ordinary linguistic representation to “the limits of language” as conditions governing philosophical possibility is not a sequence of routine deductions. 

The score approaches 9 because several major transitions—picture relation, saying/showing, logical tautology, subject-as-limit, ladder self-application—are mutually supporting yet highly non-obvious. I stop at 8.8 because portions of the later system are presented aphoristically rather than reconstructed with enough evidential constraint to make the entire inferential route demonstrably an exceptional 9-level reconstruction.

**Principal evidence:** the transition from propositional representation to a general diagnosis of the limits and proper method of philosophy.

### **IC — Integrative Compression: 9.0**

**Bounding anchor:** 9 satisfied.

The protocol's 9 anchor requires very large heterogeneous complexity to be reorganized through remarkably economical architecture while preserving unusually fine structure and enabling extensive reconstruction.  Brevity itself earns nothing; the issue is reconstructive economy.

This is perhaps the artifact's strongest dimension. A relatively small number of interacting structural principles—fact, picture, logical form, proposition, truth-operation, and saying/showing—coordinate ontology, semantics, logic, inference, modality, language, subjectivity, ethics, aesthetics, metaphysics, and philosophical method. Much of the later work can be reconstructed as consequences or boundary cases of those principles. The hierarchical numbering itself makes the intended dependency structure unusually explicit. 

This is not compression by deletion alone: the architecture preserves distinctions between empirical propositions, tautologies, contradictions, philosophical elucidations, ethical discourse, and what merely shows itself. 

**Principal evidence:** enormous conceptual range organized through a small recurrent architecture of logical representation.

### **GR — Generalization Reach: 8.4**

**Bounding anchors:** 8 satisfied; 9 not fully satisfied.

The protocol's 8 anchor requires substantial power across markedly heterogeneous cases with limited reconstruction; 9 requires successful transfer across exceptionally broad and structurally distant problem-spaces with little ad hoc modification.  Crucially, claimed universality is not itself GR.

The transferable structure is identifiable: analyze whether a representation has determinate conditions of sense; distinguish representational content from the formal conditions permitting representation; distinguish what a system can state internally from what is manifested in its functioning; reject attempts to treat framework conditions as ordinary objects inside that same framework.

Within the artifact this structure travels across ordinary propositions, logical inference, mathematics, scientific laws, solipsism, subjectivity, ethics, aesthetics, metaphysics, and philosophical method.  This securely supports 8.

I do not assign 9 because many of those destinations remain within a related philosophical-logical problem-space. The protocol specifically cautions that broad relevance does not establish structural transfer across markedly distant domains. 

**Principal evidence:** repeated application of the same representational-boundary architecture across logic, language, self, value, mathematics, and philosophical method.
