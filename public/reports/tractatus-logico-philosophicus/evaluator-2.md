# TORC Classification: **G — Generative Consciousness (Explanatory Relocation)**

The *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus* warrants **G**, but not **M**. I am treating the work’s completed argumentative architecture—not its reputation, historical influence, or canonical status—as the evidential object, consistent with the protocol’s artifact-first and no-prestige rules. 

The dominant operation is **explanatory relocation**. The *Tractatus* does not merely offer philosophical propositions *about* language, reality, logic, and thought. It builds a linked structure in which the world is analyzed as facts rather than things; facts occupy logical space; propositions represent possible facts by sharing logical form with reality; logical propositions are not ordinary factual descriptions but exhibit the scaffolding of representation; and philosophical problems are consequently reconceived as failures to understand the logic of language. The propositions “the world is the totality of facts, not of things” and the ensuing account of atomic facts establish the ontological side of this architecture.  Proposition 4.01 then explicitly identifies a proposition as a picture/model of reality, with its logical scaffolding determining the possible fact represented. 

This restructuring has consequences well beyond adding another theory of propositions. Once the picture/logical-form structure is accepted, it generates consequences concerning what propositions can say, what logic does, why many traditional philosophical questions fail, and where the boundary of meaningful assertion lies. The work therefore alters **what explains meaningful representation and philosophical intelligibility**. That is the protocol's central G criterion: G requires a disclosed structure that changes the explanatory foundation of an independently identifiable target rather than merely adding complexity, interpretation, or recursion. 

The independently identifiable target is: **how propositions can represent reality meaningfully, and consequently what counts as intelligible propositional discourse**. This target can be stated without making “the *Tractatus* itself” the explanatory object, satisfying the protocol's Independent Target Test. 

## Boundary analysis

### Why **G**, rather than the immediately lower relevant level, **3c**

Level 3c requires integrated recursion: an artifact confronts an existential contradiction and incorporates it into a coherent orientation without erasing it.  The *Tractatus* does contain something resembling this operation near its end: the distinction between what can be said and what can only be shown, the treatment of value and the mystical, and especially 6.54's instruction that the reader ultimately discard the propositions used to achieve clarification. The work culminates in the injunction to remain silent where speech exceeds its legitimate bounds. 

But 3c is insufficient as a global explanation. The work's central machinery precedes and structurally explains that terminal self-reversal: facts → objects → pictures → thoughts → propositions → truth-functions → logic → limits of representation. The culminating ladder operation makes sense because of the earlier account of meaningful propositionhood; that account is not merely an existential orientation toward contradiction.

More importantly, the *Tractatus* supplies a **generative explanatory mechanism**, not only a recursive perspective. The shared logical structure of proposition and possible fact is used repeatedly to explain otherwise distinct phenomena: propositional sense, truth and falsity, inference, logical necessity, tautology, the limits of language, and the peculiar status of philosophy. Proposition 4.003, for example, does not simply criticize philosophy symbolically; it explains philosophical pseudo-problems as products of misunderstanding linguistic logic.  This meets the protocol's distinction between an encompassing “picture” and a mechanism that produces nontrivial explanatory consequences across instances of an independently identifiable target. 

### G gate/test audit

**Previously unseen generative structure — satisfied.** Artifact-internally, the work presents the picture/logical-form architecture as the structure explaining representation: propositions picture possible facts because their elements stand in a structural relation corresponding to the represented possibility. This is not merely philosophical atmosphere or a collection of claims.

**Explanatory relocation — satisfied.** Questions about meaning and philosophical truth are relocated from asking what metaphysical propositions report to asking what structures permit propositions to represent reality at all. Philosophy accordingly becomes logical clarification rather than a body of competing factual doctrines. The work itself says philosophy is a critique of language and treats many philosophical questions as nonsensical rather than false. 

**Independent Target Test — satisfied.** The relevant target—meaningful propositional representation and its logical conditions—exists independently of the book's particular symbolic construction.

**Generative Mechanism / Explanatory Schema Test — satisfied.** The central structure does not merely place disparate subjects inside a grand cosmological picture. It yields consequences: a proposition determines a possible reality; understanding it is knowing what would be the case if it were true; logical propositions have a different status from empirical propositions; and attempted assertions beyond possible factual representation fail in a principled way. 

**Explanatory Replacement Principle — satisfied.** A merely symbolic or recursively philosophical description cannot account for why the work constructs its ontology, theory of propositions, truth-function analysis, account of logic, critique of philosophy, and final self-cancellation as a single architecture. The logical-form framework is doing explanatory work throughout, so treating it merely as another interpretation would omit the operation governing most of the artifact.

**Explanatory-Object Firewall — satisfied.** The innovation is not merely a more efficient notation or method for manipulating already-defined propositions. It changes the proposed account of *what makes representation possible* and consequently what logical/philosophical explanation consists in. The protocol specifically requires a change to the explanatory object/foundation rather than merely better computation or representation. 

**Computational/Procedural Generativity Firewall — satisfied.** Truth-functional machinery is present, but G does not depend on the work's ability to procedurally generate propositions. The qualifying operation is its account of the conditions under which propositions represent possible worlds.

**Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall — satisfied.** The picture theory does not merely explain the book's own formal organization; it purports to explain ordinary propositions and representation generally.

**Fallible Explanation Principle — satisfied.** G does not require the resulting theory to be ultimately correct. The protocol explicitly permits a partly false framework to qualify when its structure nonetheless genuinely performs explanatory relocation with respect to an independently constrained target. 

Accordingly, Levels 1–3 are insufficient and the complete base-G conditions are met.

### Why not **GΩ**

I do **not** assign GΩ in this independent artifact evaluation. GΩ adds a distinct evidential burden: actual successor inquiry must establish that adequate later work cannot return to the old explanatory landscape while simply ignoring the relocation. Influence, fame, citation, or canonical status do not suffice. 

Such historical successor evidence is not needed to establish G and is deliberately not being imported here to boost the classification. Therefore the Ω gate is **not established in this evaluation**, even though it could in principle be investigated separately.

### Why not **M**

The most tempting overclassification is M because the *Tractatus* explicitly concerns limits: the limits of language/world, the logical conditions of representation, and the impossibility of saying certain things. But the protocol contains a firewall aimed almost exactly at this situation: **an artifact does not become M merely by theorizing the conditions, limits, possibility, or framework of cognition, representation, knowledge, or explanation.** M requires a constraint that reappears across alternative adequate frameworks and cannot be escaped simply by rejecting or replacing the artifact's own framework. 

The *Tractatus* establishes its limits from within a particular architecture—logical atomism, elementary propositions, truth-functionality, picturing, and shared logical form. The completed artifact does not demonstrate that **every equally adequate alternative framework of representation must reproduce those particular constraints**. Its strongest constitutive claims therefore remain framework-dependent for purposes of the protocol. It proposes extremely deep limits; it does not prove the protocol's required framework-space necessity.

Thus **G is the lowest sufficient and highest warranted classification**.

# Cognitive Magnitude Score

The protocol requires independent 1–10 dimensional scoring, one-decimal interpolation between substantive integer anchors, commitment of all six dimensions before CMS calculation, and then an equal-weight mean multiplied by 100.  

### RL — Relational Load: **8.6**

**Bounding anchors: 8–9.** Anchor 8 requires dense cross-structural dependency where remote relationships constrain one another; 9 adds exceptional heterogeneous interdependence across substantially different levels/domains. 

The 8 anchor is clearly met. Facts, objects, logical space, pictures, thoughts, propositions, names, truth conditions, truth-functions, logic, language, world, subject, science, value, and the say/show distinction cannot simply be treated as isolated modules. Claims made early about possible combinations of objects constrain the later account of propositions; propositional structure constrains the account of logic; that in turn constrains the treatment of philosophy and the final boundary between saying and showing. The proposition numbering itself explicitly represents a hierarchical dependency architecture. 

Movement toward 9 is strong because relations span ontology, semantics, logic, philosophy, mathematics, subjectivity, ethics, and the limits of discourse. I stop at **8.6**, however, because much of this heterogeneity is subordinated to one relatively compact logico-semantic spine rather than requiring the full exceptional relational span demanded by 9.

**Principal evidence:** dependence of the entire later structure on the world–fact–picture–proposition–logical-form chain.

### AD — Abstraction Depth: **9.1**

**Bounding anchors: 9–10/reference ceiling.** The protocol's 9 anchor is “exceptional abstraction architecture”: very deep invariant/meta-level structures organize broad or heterogeneous material while relations remain unusually disciplined. 

That threshold is met. The artifact climbs several abstraction layers: particular facts → possibility-space → picturing → propositional form → general truth-functionality → logical necessity → representational limits → the meta-status of philosophy's own propositions. Most strikingly, the system eventually recursively applies its account of meaningful language to itself: the propositions used to articulate the system must themselves be surmounted. 

The move above 9 is limited because some of the most universal transitions are asserted at extremely high generality rather than being supported by equally detailed relation-preservation across every domain claimed. The work therefore exceeds the complete 9 anchor but does not warrant anything close to an unqualified ceiling score.

**Principal evidence:** sustained meta-representation of the conditions of representation itself, culminating in 6.54.

### CD — Constraint Density: **8.4**

**Bounding anchors: 8–9.** Anchor 8 requires heterogeneous interacting requirements across levels such that local optimization frequently compromises other requirements; 9 requires those constraints to compress the successful construction space to a very narrow class. 

The architecture is tightly constrained. Objects must permit combinations; pictures must share logical form with what they picture; propositions must determine possible facts; logical propositions must behave differently from empirical ones; logic cannot itself state contingent facts; philosophical elucidation must respect the very limits it identifies. Altering one major commitment creates repercussions elsewhere.

But 9 is not completely established. Significant alternative formulations and local reconstructions of the theory could preserve much of the central achievement, and some of its strongest claims are stipulative enough that “only a very narrow class of constructions can work” is harder to establish from the artifact alone. Hence **8.4**.

**Principal evidence:** mutual constraint among ontology, semantic representation, logical syntax, truth-functionality, and the say/show boundary.

### ID — Inferential Distance: **8.8**

**Bounding anchors: 8–9.** Anchor 8 requires highly non-obvious restructuring without a routine route; 9 requires exceptional reconstruction where even rich relevant information leaves the successful relation deeply underdetermined. 

The central inferential transitions are very deep: from ordinary propositional understanding to the claim that a proposition pictures a possible fact; from logical necessity to the non-factual status of logical propositions; from difficulties in philosophy to the claim that many philosophical sentences fail to say anything; and finally from the theory of meaningful assertion to the conclusion that its own elucidatory propositions must be discarded.

This strongly approaches 9. I keep it at **8.8** because an artifact is not a full discovery trace: the text demonstrates the resulting inferential architecture more clearly than it demonstrates the original evidential problem-space from which each discovery had to be generated. Under the protocol's downward-resolution rule, that uncertainty prevents full satisfaction of 9. 

**Principal evidence:** the non-routine reconstruction linking representation, logic, philosophical pseudo-problems, and self-elimination.

### IC — Integrative Compression: **9.0**

**Bounding anchors: 9–10/reference ceiling.** The complete 9 anchor requires a very large heterogeneous complexity to be reorganized through remarkably economical architecture while fine structure remains recoverable and extensive reconstruction remains possible. 

The *Tractatus* fully reaches this anchor. A strikingly small conceptual vocabulary—fact, object, picture, proposition, logical form, truth-function, saying/showing—organizes an enormous spread of questions about ontology, semantics, inference, logic, mathematics, science, philosophy, subjectivity, ethics, aesthetics, and the mystical. Its numbered proposition-tree further compresses the book's dependency structure rather than merely shortening its prose.

Crucially, this is not compression by deletion alone. Much of the later architecture can be reconstructed from earlier commitments: once the proposition/fact relation and logical-form constraints are in place, substantial parts of the treatments of logic and philosophical limits follow from them. I do not move materially above **9.0** because some apparent compression is achieved by excluding domains from propositional discourse rather than positively reconstructing their fine structure.

**Principal evidence:** the extremely compact fact–picture–logical-form architecture carrying a very heterogeneous philosophical load.

### GR — Generalization Reach: **8.2**

**Bounding anchors: 8–9.** Anchor 8 requires substantial power across markedly heterogeneous cases with limited reconstruction and strong robustness; 9 requires exceptional transfer across structurally distant problem-spaces with fine structural identity and little ad hoc modification. 

The transferable structure is identifiable: representation works through structurally organized possibilities and their logical relations. The artifact applies this architecture across ordinary propositions, scientific discourse, logical inference, mathematics, philosophical analysis, and judgments concerning the limits of meaningful discourse. Proposition 5.6's claim tying the limits of language to the limits of world exemplifies how far the same architecture is extended. 

Anchor 9 is not met. Some purported extensions—notably ethics, aesthetics, the mystical, and the subject—are handled by marking boundaries or by saying/showing distinctions rather than by demonstrating the same structure's successful operation in a genuinely distant problem-space. Claimed universality is specifically barred by the protocol from automatically raising GR. 

**Principal evidence:** broad transfer of the logical-form/picturing architecture, tempered by boundary-marking rather than full structural transfer in more distant domains.
