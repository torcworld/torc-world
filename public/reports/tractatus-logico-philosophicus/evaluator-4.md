## 1. Operational Order (TORC)

**G — Generative Consciousness / Explanatory Relocation**

The highest warranted classification is **G**.

The dominant operation of *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus* is not merely symbolic representation or recursive reflection on consciousness and reality. Its central construction attempts to explain **how meaningful propositional representation is possible at all** by relocating explanation into a structural relation among world, facts, pictures/propositions, and logical form. Its core sequence—world as facts rather than things; pictures sharing logical form with what they depict; propositions as logical pictures; propositions generated truth-functionally from elementary propositions; logic as showing rather than stating the formal structure of reality—forms a single explanatory architecture.

That matters under this protocol because G requires more than abstraction, universality, or philosophical ambition: it requires a structure that changes **what explains an independently identifiable target**, and the structure must generate explanatory consequences rather than merely rearrange the target within a novel picture.  

Here the independently identifiable target is:

**the capacity of propositions/thought to represent reality, together with the distinctive status of logical necessity and the limits of meaningful propositional expression.**

The artifact's answer is not simply another interpretation of particular propositions. Representation works because proposition and possible fact possess corresponding logical structure; truth-functional articulation then explains the organization of complex propositions, while the same architecture generates the distinction between factual saying and logical showing. The explanatory basis is therefore shifted from the content of individual representations to their **formal possibility of representation**.

### G gate analysis

The protocol requires an independent target; an internally generated symbolic world cannot itself serve as the target.  The *Tractatus* passes this test: ordinary propositional representation, truth/falsity, inference, and logical necessity are phenomena identifiable independently of the book's terminology.

**Previously unseen generative structure: Pass.** The operative structure disclosed by the artifact is the coordinated picture/logical-form/truth-functional architecture. Its explanatory role is not reducible to the individual doctrines composing it.

**Explanatory relocation: Pass.** What makes representation possible is relocated to shared formal structure between proposition and possible fact. Logical necessity is correspondingly relocated away from a special body of truths about the world toward the formal conditions under which propositions represent anything.

**Explanatory Replacement Principle: Pass.** A merely symbolic or recursively philosophical description fails to explain why the book systematically derives consequences concerning depiction, sense, logical form, elementary propositions, tautology, contradiction, inference, and limits of saying from one underlying architecture. G requires the prior framework to become insufficient rather than merely supplemented. 

**Generative mechanism vs. explanatory schema: Pass, though this is the closest gate.** The architecture does more than arrange language and reality in an elegant ontology. Shared logical form is used to explain *why* propositions possess determinate possibilities of truth and falsity; the elementary-proposition/truth-function construction then generates consequences for complex propositions and logic. This meets the protocol's requirement that the candidate structure produce nontrivial explanatory consequences across instances of the independent target. 

**Explanatory-Object Firewall: Pass.** The achievement is not merely an improved notation or method of logical calculation. The artifact proposes an account of **what explains representation and logical necessity themselves**. The protocol explicitly distinguishes such a transformation from improved manipulation of an already-defined target. 

**Computational/Procedural Generativity Firewall: Pass.** Truth-functional generation by itself would not establish G; procedural generation explicitly cannot do so.  The G case instead rests on the larger representational mechanism—logical picturing and shared logical form—of which truth-functional construction is one consequence.

**Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall: Pass.** The system is not credited merely because its propositions form an internally elegant formal architecture. Its intended explanatory object—actual meaningful representation—is external to that architecture.

**Fallible Explanation Principle: Pass.** G does not require ultimate truth, but neither may explanatory success rest solely on internal coherence or definitional closure.  The *Tractatus* generates externally assessable consequences: meaningful propositions must articulate possibilities of fact; logical propositions do not describe contingent states of affairs; certain attempted propositions fail to state what they appear to state. These claims can succeed or fail against linguistic/logical phenomena independently of accepting the book's vocabulary.

Thus all four stated G necessary conditions are satisfied: generative structure, relocation of explanatory foundation, insufficiency of lower orders, and successful organization of the target domain. 

### Boundary analysis: why G rather than 3c

A 3c reading can explain important portions of the artifact, especially the reflexive conclusion in which its own propositions are eventually treated as something to be overcome, together with the distinction between what can be said and what can only show itself. But **integrated recursive consciousness does not sufficiently explain the book's global operation**.

Remove the picture/logical-form/truth-functional theory and the major argumentative structure collapses. Remove the existentially integrative or ladder-like ending, and a large explanatory system concerning propositions, facts, logic, and representation still remains. The generative explanatory architecture therefore dominates the recursive component.

G is also categorically distinct from simply maximizing Level-3 abstraction or complexity: the protocol states that no accumulation of recursion, abstraction, ingenuity, or complexity is sufficient for G; G requires relocation of explanation itself.  The *Tractatus* warrants G because of that relocation, not because it is difficult or philosophically ambitious.

### Why not GΩ

**GΩ is not established.**

The protocol makes GΩ a separate evidential judgment requiring **actual independent successor inquiry or practice** showing that the explanatory relocation became an unavoidable baseline for adequate successor work; fame, citation, canonical status, or influence are explicitly insufficient. 

Nothing intrinsic to the completed artifact can by itself establish this historical/domain-structural successor condition. Under the requested independent artifact evaluation, therefore, the Ω burden is unmet. I do not promote the work merely because later philosophy famously engaged with it. The required claim would be stronger: that adequate successor inquiry **cannot** simply bypass the transformed problem structure. 

For completeness, **M is also not warranted**. The book explicitly theorizes limits on meaningful language and the conditions of representation, but the M firewall says that theorizing the conditions or limits of cognition, representation, knowledge, or explanation does **not** itself establish M. M requires a constraint that reappears across alternative adequate frameworks and cannot be escaped simply by rejecting the artifact's own framework.  The *Tractatus* does not demonstrate that every equally adequate theory of linguistic representation must preserve its picture-theoretic ontology or its particular say/show boundary.

## 2. Cognitive Magnitude Score

The dimensional profile is committed independently before calculating CMS, as required by the protocol. Decimal scores interpolate between substantive integer anchors and cannot be tuned retrospectively to obtain a desired aggregate. 

**RL — 8.8**

The artifact demonstrates dense cross-structural dependency: ontology of objects/states of affairs constrains the account of pictures; picture structure constrains propositions and thought; logical form links representation to possible reality; elementary propositions constrain truth-functional composition; these jointly constrain logic and the limits of saying. This fully clears RL8, where remote relations materially constrain one another and simultaneous coordination is central. 

It approaches RL9 because the relations span ontology, semantics, logic, thought, and metalinguistic limits. I stop at **8.8** because the heterogeneous regions are not uniformly mutually interdependent: the ethical/aesthetic/mystical material at the end is linked chiefly through the say/show boundary rather than through the same densely articulated dependency network.

**Principal evidence:** the dependence chain facts → pictures → logical form → propositions → elementary propositions → truth functions → logic → say/show limit.

**AD — 9.2**

The artifact fully demonstrates the protocol's AD9 anchor: exceptionally deep invariant/meta-level structure organizing broad material under disciplined relation-preservation.  It does not merely abstract from propositions; it asks for the invariant structural conditions under which *any proposition could represent*, then ascends again to what can and cannot itself be propositionally represented.

The movement beyond 9 is modest rather than near-maximal because several purportedly universal transitions depend upon strong simplifying assumptions—most importantly elementary propositions and simple objects—rather than preserving fine structural distinctions across every form of actual language.

**Principal evidence:** logical form as an invariant of world/picture/proposition; the meta-distinction between saying formal structure and showing it; the final self-application of that limit.

**CD — 8.5**

The system satisfies the dense coupled-constraint architecture of CD8.  A successful construction must simultaneously maintain correspondence between world and representation, compositionality, logical possibility, independence of elementary propositions, truth functionality, the special status of logical propositions, and the say/show limit. Changing one major commitment propagates through much of the architecture.

It does not fully demonstrate CD9's extremely narrow class of viable constructions. A substantial portion of the system is supported by asserted structural necessities whose exact realization is not uniquely forced; alternative semantic and ontological constructions can preserve significant portions of the achievement.

**Principal evidence:** mutually restrictive demands placed on objects, names, elementary propositions, logical form, truth conditions, logic, and meaningful sayability.

**ID — 8.7**

The artifact fully satisfies ID8: it performs several highly non-obvious restructurings with no routine route.  The transitions from world-as-totality-of-facts to representation-as-picturing, from representability to shared logical form, and from logic as truths to logic as tautological manifestation of formal structure are substantial conceptual leaps.

It approaches but does not fully establish ID9. The protocol requires exceptional inferential reconstruction from rich information that nevertheless leaves the successful relation deeply underdetermined. Some of the *Tractatus*' largest leaps are **asserted architectonically rather than reconstructively compelled**; their originality/non-obviousness is clearer than their full inferential derivation.

**Principal evidence:** the non-routine reconstruction linking ontology, representation and logic; discounted because key transitions are compressed into propositions rather than exhaustively derived.

**IC — 8.8**

The artifact clearly reaches IC8: a remarkably compact architecture organizes a large heterogeneous field with considerable reconstructive power.  A small family of ideas—fact, picture, logical form, proposition, truth-function, say/show—organizes ontology, semantics, logic, thought, philosophical method, and limits of discourse.

It comes close to IC9, but the protocol requires unusually fine structural preservation in addition to economy. The compression is sometimes purchased by aggressively simplifying the phenomena: natural-language complexity, semantic variation, and the assumed decomposition into elementary propositions are not preserved with enough fine-grained structure to make IC9 fully secure. The protocol specifically warns that brevity alone is not compression. 

**Principal evidence:** enormous explanatory economy of the picture/logical-form architecture, tempered by losses introduced through idealization.

**GR — 7.4**

The transferable structure is identifiable: analyze representational systems through the relation between structured representation, possible represented configuration, combinatorial form, and conditions of meaningful articulation. This satisfies GR7's reliable transfer across substantially varied cases.  It applies across propositions, thought, formal logic, depiction/modeling, and philosophical analysis of meaningful discourse.

It does not reach GR8 because the transfer is substantially strongest **within one related ontology of representation and propositional structure**. Apparent extension to ethics, aesthetics, value, and the mystical occurs largely by assigning them to the boundary of propositionally expressible content rather than demonstrating the same architecture functioning robustly inside those structurally distant domains. The protocol explicitly warns that claimed universality and broad relevance are not equivalent to structural transfer. 

**Principal evidence:** strong transfer of the representational architecture across logical/semantic cases, but limited demonstrated fidelity across genuinely heterogeneous problem-spaces.
