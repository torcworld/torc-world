## Operational Order (TORC): **Level 3b — Fractured Recursive Consciousness**

Treating *Illuminations* as the completed poetic collection rather than as Rimbaud’s reputation, biography, historical influence, or later reception, the highest warranted classification is **3b**.

The collection’s dominant operation exceeds ordinary symbolic consciousness because its recurrent transformations of city, body, nature, temporality, social order, identity, and language do not function merely as symbols *for* an otherwise stable represented reality. Across poems such as “Vies,” “Ville,” “Métropolitain,” “Barbare,” “Being Beauteous,” “Solde,” “Démocratie,” “Aube,” and “Génie,” the perceiving consciousness and the reality it encounters become mutually unstable. Perspective does not simply interpret a world; the conditions under which a world can appear coherent are themselves repeatedly placed under pressure.

That is the decisive Level-3 feature. Under the protocol, recursion begins when representation, interpretation, orientation, or “the conditions under which reality becomes intelligible” become objects of inquiry, and symbolism alone must be demonstrably insufficient.  *Illuminations* repeatedly performs precisely that destabilization rather than merely depicting strange things.

Within Level 3, **3b fits better than 3a or 3c**. The collection certainly contains social and political fractures, but its deeper recurring contradiction is between consciousness and an existence that will not settle into a single stable organization. Its characteristic operation is confrontation with unstable ontological, perceptual, linguistic, bodily, and social reality rather than a program for correcting external institutions. This matches the protocol's 3b distinction: the fracture moves from external organization into the relationship between consciousness and existence, and the dominant movement becomes confrontation rather than correction. 

### Boundary analysis

**Why 3b rather than Level 2:** Level 2 can already accommodate extremely sophisticated symbolism, ambiguity, formal experimentation, philosophical depth, and nonlinear organization; those features alone cannot establish recursion.  Here, however, removing the recursive problem leaves a major part of the collection unexplained. The incessant perceptual metamorphoses are not adequately described as a stable symbolic vocabulary in which image A represents concept B. The subject-position itself migrates; incompatible ontologies coexist; categories such as artificial/natural, human/nonhuman, city/vision, body/object, presence/absence, and waking/dreaming continually reorganize the conditions of intelligibility. Symbolic interpretation explains individual images, but it does not fully explain why the collection systematically makes *the production and instability of intelligibility* operative.

That satisfies the protocol's three-entry requirement for Level 3: symbolic organization is insufficient; a recursive operation is positively present; and the operation recurs globally rather than residing in one isolated poem. 

**Why 3b rather than 3a:** Social criticism in “Démocratie,” “Solde,” urban poems, and elsewhere is real, but correction is not the collection's governing movement. There is no stable external order whose repair would restore consciousness to reality. The fracture survives removal of particular political or social targets: perception, selfhood, embodiment, language, temporality, desire, and world remain unstable. The protocol explicitly distinguishes this from 3a's externally located corrective recursion. 

**Why not 3c:** *Illuminations* sometimes produces moments suggestive of synthesis or transfiguration—most notably the expansive affirmative structure of “Génie”—but these do not establish a **dominant global integration** of the collection's contradiction. The plurality remains volatile. The artifact does not supply a coherent existential orientation capable of containing the fracture while preserving it. A local vision of reconciliation cannot elevate the whole collection when confrontation and discontinuous transformation remain globally dominant. The protocol requires positive evidence of the additional integrative operation, not merely sophistication or isolated resolution.

Accordingly, the collection stops at **3b**. This is especially important because the protocol warns that ambiguity, fragmentation, surrealism, dream logic, and psychological instability are only signals; 3b requires the underlying existential fracture actually to survive those stylistic devices.  In *Illuminations*, I judge that it does—but that its contradictions are not globally integrated.

No G test is required because the assigned classification does not reach G. I also find no reason to advance to it: the collection's extraordinary invention reorganizes experience and representation, but that is not equivalent to relocating the explanatory foundation of an independently identifiable target. The protocol expressly rejects such inflation from abstraction, symbolic density, metaphysical scope, or claims about reality and consciousness.
