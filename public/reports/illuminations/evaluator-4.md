# TORC classification: **Level 2 — Symbolic Consciousness**

Treating *Illuminations* as the completed artifact under evaluation, the highest warranted TORC classification is **Level 2**. This is not a judgment about its literary merit. The protocol requires the lowest level sufficient to explain the dominant achieved operation and expressly forbids using canonical status, difficulty, influence, or reputation as evidence. 

The dominant operation of *Illuminations* is the transformation of represented reality into an intensely constructed symbolic reality. Across pieces such as “After the Flood,” “Childhood,” “Cities,” “Vagabonds,” “Aube,” “Marine,” “Barbarian,” “Being Beauteous,” “Parade,” “Democracy,” and “Génie,” concrete categories—city, body, landscape, machinery, childhood, commerce, religion, sexuality, political order, natural phenomena—are repeatedly detached from ordinary reference and recombined into symbolic/visionary configurations. The meaning of the individual images is therefore inseparable from their metaphorical, syntactic, tonal, and cross-domain relationships.

That operation is substantially beyond direct representation. But **symbolic organization remains sufficient to explain what the collection actually does**. The protocol specifically warns that ambiguity, abstraction, complexity, surrealism, nonlinear structure, and philosophical appearance are signals rather than proof of a higher operation. 

The collection repeatedly destabilizes perception, selfhood, reference, social reality, and the distinction between inner and outer worlds. Yet much of that destabilization is accomplished *by* the constructed visionary language. There is insufficient artifact-internal evidence that the dominant global operation is the Level-3 act of making the relationship between consciousness and reality itself the sustained object of investigation, rather than producing radically transformed symbolic realities through which such questions can arise.

A useful diagnostic annotation is therefore **Level 2 with strong Level-3 appearance**. Under the protocol, this distinction matters: symbolic or philosophical sophistication cannot itself manufacture recursive consciousness. 

## Boundary analysis

### Why Level 2 rather than Level 1

Level 1 cannot adequately explain the artifact. The represented objects in *Illuminations* rarely function principally as ordinary objects or events. Cities become impossible composite structures; landscapes combine natural, architectural, technological, theatrical, and bodily properties; speakers and perceptual positions shift without realistic continuity; political and social language becomes grotesquely theatrical; and physical perception repeatedly becomes a vehicle for transformations extending beyond the represented scene.

The collection consequently requires symbolic relations to explain its dominant operation. Remove those relations and a substantial portion of the artifact's operative meaning disappears. Direct representation is therefore insufficient.

### Why not Level 3a

There is genuine social and political fracture—most conspicuously in pieces such as “Democracy,” and elsewhere in representations of commerce, bourgeois order, modern cities, labor, authority, and civilization.

But **correction is not the collection's dominant movement**. *Illuminations* does not globally identify an external social/moral/institutional disorder and recursively organize itself around repairing it. The political material is one strand inside the larger visionary-symbolic architecture.

So 3a is not the appropriate recursive classification.

### Why not Level 3b

This is the serious boundary.

*Illuminations* repeatedly stages unstable consciousness confronting an unstable world: identity fragments; ordinary causality disappears; subject and environment interpenetrate; familiar systems of meaning cease to provide secure orientation. Those properties make **fractured recursive consciousness** a plausible candidate.

But the protocol's Existential Resistance requirement is decisive. For 3b, existential fracture must survive removal of the artifact's “stylistic distortions, symbolic constructions, and formal experimentation”; strangeness, fragmentation, dream logic, ambiguity, and psychological instability do not themselves establish the operation. 

That test is not securely passed here. Much of the apparent ontological fracture in *Illuminations* is generated precisely through radical metaphor, impossible spatial conjunction, syntactic discontinuity, perceptual mutation, and symbolic recombination. Strip those mechanisms away and it becomes difficult to identify a single independently disclosed existential contradiction governing the collection as a whole.

There are individual passages and poems that approach 3b. But TORC requires the proposed operation to govern the **completed artifact globally**, not merely appear locally. The protocol also resolves unresolved higher-level evidence downward. 

Accordingly, **Level 2 remains the lowest sufficient classification**.

### Why not G

G is even less well supported. The extraordinary invention of *Illuminations* should not be confused with explanatory relocation.

No independently identifiable target domain can be specified for which the collection establishes a generative mechanism that explains why distinct target phenomena take the forms they do. Instead, the artifact creates extraordinary symbolic and perceptual organizations. Under the protocol, artifact-internal generativity, novel ontology, abstraction, or a new representational world does not suffice for G; the relevant structure must relocate the explanatory foundation of an independently identifiable target. 

Thus its enormous representational inventiveness does not warrant G.

# Cognitive Magnitude Score

I score the dimensions independently before calculating CMS, as required. The protocol requires evidence → integer anchor → interpolation → committed profile → mechanical CMS, and prohibits adjusting dimensions to make the aggregate look appropriate. 

### RL — Relational Load: **6.6**

**Anchor interval: 6–7.**

The collection demonstrates strong relational load. Within individual illuminations, visual imagery, spatial organization, speaker position, syntax, sound, social vocabulary, technological imagery, natural phenomena, and symbolic associations frequently constrain one another. Local paraphrase consequently loses substantial structure.

The movement toward 7 comes from the number of nonlocal and cross-category relations that must be held together: organic/mechanical, sacred/commercial, bodily/architectural, natural/artificial, intimate/civic, and subjective/objective structures repeatedly intersect.

I stop at **6.6**, however, because the collection is materially decomposable. Individual illuminations possess substantial autonomy, and the absence of a securely demonstrated global sequential architecture weakens the case that numerous remote relations throughout the entire corpus must be maintained simultaneously. This fits the protocol's distinction between strong relational load (6) and advanced nonlocal dependency (7). 

**Principal evidence:** dense intra-poem cross-domain relations; recurring transformations of landscape/city/body/technology; substantial independence among individual pieces.

### AD — Abstraction Depth: **7.7**

**Anchor interval: 7–8.**

The artifact fully demonstrates advanced abstraction. Concrete scenes repeatedly become structures for relations among perception, identity, modernity, desire, civilization, nature, transcendence, violence, and representation itself. Multiple abstraction levels are often active simultaneously.

It moves strongly toward 8 because these transformations cross considerable representational distance while preserving recognizable structural correspondences: cities remain cities even while becoming impossible conceptual spaces; bodies remain bodies while merging with aesthetic or environmental systems; natural scenes preserve spatial relations while being transformed into visionary structures.

I stop short of 8 because structural correspondence is deliberately unstable in substantial portions of the collection. Some transitions achieve suggestive multiplicity rather than the strongly disciplined relation-preservation required for the complete 8 anchor. The protocol explicitly prevents obscurity, symbolism, or philosophical appearance from substituting for constrained abstraction. 

**Principal evidence:** sustained movement from concrete perception through symbolic structure to meta-perceptual and ontological implications.

### CD — Constraint Density: **6.8**

**Anchor interval: 6–7.**

Successful constructions must simultaneously coordinate lexical precision, syntactic rhythm, prose/poetic form, abrupt image transitions, perceptual continuity, tonal movement, symbolic resonance, and recurring conceptual oppositions. Changing a crucial image or syntactic relation can disrupt several functions simultaneously.

This substantially satisfies the 6 anchor and approaches 7.

The score remains below 7 because the protocol asks how narrow the space of successful constructions actually is, rather than how intricate the resulting artifact appears. *Illuminations* permits considerable substitution, rearrangement, and local variation while retaining its central visionary-symbolic operation. Complexity alone cannot establish extreme constraint density. 

**Principal evidence:** simultaneous poetic, syntactic, imagistic, rhythmic, and conceptual requirements, counterbalanced by considerable local construction freedom.

### ID — Inferential Distance: **7.6**

**Anchor interval: 7–8.**

The artifact demonstrates advanced non-routine relation-finding. Its characteristic transitions are rarely supplied by ordinary descriptive procedure: urban architecture becomes anatomy or spectacle; landscape acquires technological structure; apparently incompatible semantic fields suddenly reveal usable relations; perceptual categories are repeatedly reorganized rather than merely decorated.

Movement toward 8 is substantial because many successful conjunctions have numerous plausible alternatives and no routine derivational route.

I do not assign 8 because some of the apparent distance comes from unconstrained associative possibility. The protocol distinguishes genuine inferential distance from surprise, search-space size, and unsupported leaps.  The strongest passages make their unexpected relations retrospectively compelling; not every transition in the collection achieves that degree of necessity.

**Principal evidence:** highly non-obvious cross-domain metaphorical reconstruction and perceptual/category shifts.

### IC — Integrative Compression: **7.5**

**Anchor interval: 7–8.**

This is one of the artifact's strongest dimensions. Individual pieces can organize extraordinary heterogeneity—architecture, color, sound, technology, bodies, social structures, mythology, landscape, commerce, violence, eroticism—through extremely compact representational architectures.

The complete 7 anchor is satisfied: substantial heterogeneous complexity is economically organized while important distinctions remain recoverable. The work moves significantly toward 8 because remarkably short structures can sustain several interacting interpretive systems.

The principal limitation is global. The collection as a whole does not clearly reduce its heterogeneous material to one compact architecture with the reconstructive power demanded by the full 8 anchor. Its plurality is partly irreducible. The protocol specifically distinguishes genuine compression from mere brevity.  

**Principal evidence:** exceptionally economical integration within individual prose poems, offset by weaker collection-wide compression.

### GR — Generalization Reach: **6.4**

**Anchor interval: 6–7.**

The transferable architecture is identifiable: ordinary perceptual and conceptual categories can be recombined so that apparently familiar reality becomes intelligible through new relations. That operation successfully traverses substantially different immediate materials—nature, cities, childhood, political order, technology, bodies, desire, travel, religion, and aesthetic experience.

This establishes strong generalization across a broad class of cases.

I stop at **6.4** because those cases remain predominantly within a common human-perceptual, social, and visionary ontology. The protocol explicitly warns that broad relevance to human/social experience is not equivalent to transfer across markedly heterogeneous problem-spaces. 

**Principal evidence:** recurrence of the same transformational architecture across widely varied subject matter, with limited evidence for structurally distant domain transfer.
