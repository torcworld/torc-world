# 1. Operational Order (TORC)

**G — Generative Consciousness: Explanatory Relocation**

*War and Peace* warrants **G** because its highest successful dominant operation is not merely symbolic representation of war, society, love, death, and history, nor merely recursive integration of existential contradiction. Across the completed work—especially through the recurrent treatment of battles, command, causation, Napoleon, Kutuzov, individual intention, contingency, and the historical essays culminating in the epilogue—the novel progressively displaces an intuitive explanatory model in which history is made by identifiable “great” agents and replaces it with a distributed model in which historical outcomes arise from innumerable interacting actions, conditions, constraints, and necessities that no individual consciousness commands.

That matters because TORC defines G categorically: complexity or profundity cannot produce G; the work must **change the explanatory basis through which an independently identifiable target becomes intelligible**.  The candidate target here can be stated independently of the novel: **large-scale historical causation, especially the relation between individual agency and collective historical events**. The novel does more than depict that problem. It repeatedly makes predictions/explanatory consequences from the replacement framework: orders fail to cause what commanders believe they cause; battlefield outcomes emerge from decentralized local actions; retrospective histories falsely assign causal unity to salient individuals; apparent freedom changes with scale and perspective; and causal attribution becomes less adequate as one moves from local intention to historical totality.

This is therefore stronger than “a philosophical novel containing a theory of history.” The explanatory structure repeatedly governs how major represented events become intelligible.

The protocol requires the lowest sufficient order, with higher classifications carrying the burden of proof.  On that standard, I judge **G** to be warranted, but only moderately above the threshold.

## Boundary analysis

### Why G rather than 3c

A **3c** reading is genuinely strong. Pierre's captivity and later orientation, Andrei's confrontation with mortality, Natasha's suffering and recovery, Nikolai's maturation, and the completed domestic structures all exhibit integration of contradiction without simply abolishing mortality, suffering, contingency, or limitation. That closely matches the protocol's definition of 3c as preserving existential contradiction while incorporating it into a coherent orientation.

But 3c is insufficient to explain a major global structure of the completed artifact: the persistent dismantling and replacement of the novel's model of **historical explanation**. The Battle of Austerlitz, Borodino, Moscow's abandonment and burning, the French retreat, the contrast between Napoleon and Kutuzov, the treatment of military orders, and finally the epilogue are not adequately explained simply as existential integration. They cumulatively establish an account of why historical events assume forms irreducible to the intentions attributed to their supposed makers.

That exceeds recursive consciousness. The protocol's dividing line is explicit: Level 3 transforms consciousness *within* a problem-space; G transforms **what explains the problem-space or target**. 

### Explicit G gate/test analysis

**Independent Target Test — PASS.**

The target can be specified without reference to the fictional world: *causal explanation of large-scale historical events and the relation of individual agency to collective outcomes*. It is therefore not merely the novel explaining its own symbolism or narrative machinery. The protocol requires precisely this independently stateable target for G.

**Previously unseen generative structure — PASS, with qualification.**

The completed work constructs a distributed causal structure: historical events arise from enormous populations of locally motivated actions under constraints, while later explanatory narratives falsely compress this into intentional causation by prominent actors. The operation is structurally realized across narrative episodes as well as explicitly formulated in the historical sections.

“Previously unseen” here is being judged artifact-internally rather than as a claim of absolute historical priority; I am not crediting Tolstoy's reputation or supposed originality.

**Generative Mechanism vs. Explanatory Schema Test — PASS.**

The candidate structure does more than place history inside an attractive new picture. It generates repeated explanatory consequences: commanders' intentions diverge from outcomes; orders function less causally than retrospectively assumed; apparent individual authorship decreases as the scale of events rises; apparently irrational local behavior can produce intelligible aggregate outcomes; and causal stories centered on conspicuous individuals systematically misdescribe collective processes.

The protocol explicitly distinguishes a mere encompassing schema from a mechanism that explains why otherwise different target phenomena take the forms they do.  On balance, *War and Peace* clears that distinction.

**Explanatory Replacement Principle — PASS.**

The great-agent/command model is not retained as an equally sufficient account to which Tolstoy merely adds another perspective. Much of the novel is designed to make that former explanation fail. Once the distributed model is established, Napoleon's supposed control of Borodino or the occupation of Moscow becomes positively misdescribed by the lower explanatory basis.

**Explanatory-Object Firewall — PASS.**

The innovation is not merely a better literary technique for portraying war. The object being reconceived is historical causation itself. The protocol requires a change in *what explains the target*, rather than merely how it is represented or manipulated.

**Computational/Procedural Generativity Firewall — PASS.**

Nothing rests on novelty of procedure or output-generation. The relevant achievement is explanatory.

**Artifact-Internal Generativity Firewall — PASS.**

The theory does not merely explain how *War and Peace* constructs its own fictional world. Its target—historical agency and causation—is independently identifiable.

**Fallible Explanation Principle — PASS.**

The assessment does not require Tolstoy's historical theory to be ultimately correct. The protocol permits G for a fallible framework if it produces nontrivial explanatory consequences concerning an independently constrained target, rather than succeeding only by stipulation. 

**Lower-order insufficiency — PASS.**

Direct representation, symbolic organization, and integrated existential recursion explain very large parts of the work, but none alone explains why the novel systematically constructs, attacks, and replaces a theory of historical causation.

**Overall G determination: PASS.**

### Why not GΩ

**GΩ is not warranted from this evaluation.**

The protocol imposes an unusual additional requirement: GΩ cannot be established by the artifact alone. It requires **independent evidence from actual successor inquiry or practice** showing that the relocated explanatory basis became an unavoidable baseline for adequate later work. Fame, influence, canonicity, citation, and later attention are explicitly insufficient. 

No such successor-domain demonstration is contained in the artifact. I am deliberately not importing *War and Peace*'s reputation or historical influence to manufacture the Ω condition. Thus:

**G established artifact-internally: YES.**

**Successor-baseline necessity independently demonstrated here: NO.**

Therefore **G, not GΩ**.

# 2. Cognitive Magnitude Score (CMS)

I scored the six dimensions independently before aggregation. The protocol requires evidence → integer anchor → decimal interpolation, forbids prestige/exemplar anchoring, and specifically forbids tuning dimensions to obtain an intuitively attractive CMS. 

### RL — Relational Load: **9.2**

**Bounding interval: 9–10.**

The 9 anchor requires exceptional heterogeneous interdependence across substantially different levels or domains; RL10 additionally requires both **extreme interdependence and exceptional relational span**. 

*War and Peace* fully satisfies 9. Its achievement depends upon sustained relations among several major families, intimate psychology, marriages and courtship, inheritance and property, military hierarchies, battlefield action, political events, class structures, changing beliefs, temporal consequences, and the macro/micro theory of history. Remote events materially alter one another: Andrei's military experience transforms his metaphysical orientation and intimate relations; Natasha's choices affect multiple family structures; Pierre's ideological trajectories intersect historical catastrophe; military and domestic spheres repeatedly recode each other; the Napoleon/Kutuzov relation simultaneously functions historically, narratively, psychologically, and theoretically.

Movement toward 10 is real because the span is unusually heterogeneous and nonlocal. I stop at **9.2**, however, because substantial substructures remain locally intelligible and partly decomposable. Not every major relation mutually constrains every other relation to the extreme degree RL10 demands.

**Principal evidence:** interlocking Rostov/Bolkonsky/Bezukhov narratives; domestic–military causal crossover; recurrent micro/macro historical parallels; long-range psychological consequences.

---

### AD — Abstraction Depth: **8.8**

**Bounding interval: 8–9.**

The 8 anchor requires deep constrained abstraction over substantial representational distance while preserving strong structural correspondence; 9 requires very deep invariant/meta-level structures organizing broad heterogeneous material with unusually disciplined relation-preservation. 

The novel repeatedly traverses concrete event → individual perception → agency → historical causation → freedom/necessity → epistemology of historical explanation. A battlefield order is simultaneously an immediate event and evidence concerning command, agency, causal attribution, and eventually the possibility of historical knowledge. Likewise, individual experiences of mortality scale upward into questions about meaning and the limits of consciousness.

That decisively clears 8 and approaches 9. I stop just below 9 because its highest abstractions are not consistently organized by a single exceptionally deep invariant/meta-representational system: some of the historical philosophy is discursive and argumentative rather than structurally preserved through every region of the narrative.

**Principal evidence:** micro/macro treatment of agency; historical essays; freedom/necessity analysis; transformations from subjective intention to aggregate causal explanation.

---

### CD — Constraint Density: **8.6**

**Bounding interval: 8–9.**

The 8 anchor is a dense architecture of heterogeneous requirements interacting across levels; 9 requires those strongly coupled constraints to leave a **very narrow class of successful constructions**. The protocol specifically warns that sheer complexity in large narratives must not be mistaken for extreme CD and asks how much could be changed while preserving the central achievement. 

The work must simultaneously preserve historical chronology, credible character development across years, family relationships, military geography and sequence, social convention, thematic recurrence, philosophical exposition, changes of perspective, and the eventual convergence of personal and historical structures. Choices in one layer constrain several others.

That strongly satisfies 8. It moves substantially toward 9 because the major arcs are cross-constrained by chronology, psychology, history, and thematic function. But many local scenes, subsidiary figures, episodes, and sequences could be modified or replaced while the central achievement survived. The viable construction space is therefore not narrow enough for the complete 9 anchor.

**Principal evidence:** historical/narrative synchronization; psychological continuity; convergence of multiple family arcs; dependence of philosophical claims upon represented events.

---

### ID — Inferential Distance: **8.7**

**Bounding interval: 8–9.**

The protocol's 8 anchor requires highly non-obvious restructuring with meaningful alternatives and no routine route. The 9 anchor requires an exceptionally underdetermined reconstruction that remains deeply non-obvious even given rich relevant information. 

The largest demonstrated leap is from familiar intentional descriptions of historical action—leaders decide, armies obey, battles produce intended consequences—to a radically decentralized account of causal agency. The work must infer from many distinct kinds of evidence that retrospective causal attribution systematically exaggerates centralized intention. A second large inferential movement connects apparently incompatible psychological facts—freedom, necessity, mortality, contingency, suffering—to viable existential orientation.

These are deep, non-routine restructurings rather than long chains of ordinary deductions, so 8 is clearly exceeded. I stop at **8.7** because the completed artifact supplies extensive narrative evidence and increasingly explicit philosophical guidance toward its conclusion; the reconstruction is therefore less radically underdetermined than the complete 9 anchor requires.

**Principal evidence:** reconstruction of historical causality from battlefield discrepancies; reinterpretation of command; relation between local freedom and large-scale necessity; existential transformations of Pierre and Andrei.

---

### IC — Integrative Compression: **8.3**

**Bounding interval: 8–9.**

The 8 anchor requires a comparatively compact architecture organizing a large heterogeneous field with strong preservation and reconstructive power. Nine demands **remarkably economical architecture** over very large heterogeneous complexity. The protocol explicitly warns that enormous integration load alone does not establish IC9–10. 

The novel compresses an enormous range of material through a surprisingly small family of organizing relations: intention versus outcome, apparent agency versus constraint, abstraction versus lived reality, egoic self-conception versus participation in larger systems, and individual consciousness versus collective process. Those structures allow large portions of the military, domestic, historical, and philosophical material to illuminate one another.

The 9 anchor is not satisfied. *War and Peace* is highly integrative but not **remarkably economical** in the protocol's strict sense. Significant explanatory architecture remains explicitly elaborated in lengthy essays and repeated demonstrations rather than being recoverable from a maximally compact governing structure.

**Principal evidence:** recurring agency/necessity architecture linking history and private life; parallelism between personal intention and historical command; integration of domestic, military, philosophical, and psychological material.

---

### GR — Generalization Reach: **7.7**

**Bounding interval: 7–8.**

The 7 anchor requires reliable transfer across substantially varied cases with strong fidelity and identifiable boundaries; 8 requires substantial power across **markedly heterogeneous cases** with limited reconstruction. The protocol specifically warns that broad relevance within human/social experience does not automatically count as transfer across structurally distant problem-spaces. 

The transferable architecture is clear: decentralized causation, limits on intentional control, retrospective attribution of agency, interaction of local motives with aggregate outcomes, and the tension between subjective freedom and systemic constraint. It transfers naturally from warfare to politics, institutions, crowds, organizations, social coordination, and many forms of collective action. The psychological architecture likewise transfers across varied human cases.

But the strongest transfer remains within broadly **human/social/historical systems**. Applying the architecture to radically different domains would often require substantial reconstruction or become merely metaphorical. That prevents the full 8 anchor.

**Principal evidence:** the same agency–aggregate structure operating in battles, political decisions, social life, family systems, and individual action; identifiable limitation to social/historical and existential phenomena.
