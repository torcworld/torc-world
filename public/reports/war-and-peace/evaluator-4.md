## 1. Operational Order (TORC)

**Level 3c — Integrated Recursive Consciousness**

The highest warranted classification for *War and Peace* is **3c**. Its dominant global operation is not simply symbolic representation of war, society, history, love, or death. Across the completed novel, consciousness repeatedly encounters the inadequacy of its own explanatory and purposive schemes: Andrei's ambition collapses against mortality and contingency; Pierre cycles through intellectual, moral, political, and spiritual systems that fail to master lived reality; military actors discover that plans and command bear an unstable relation to actual outcomes; historical explanation itself is subjected to sustained scrutiny. The work therefore makes the relationship between consciousness, interpretation, agency, and reality an object of inquiry rather than merely representing those things symbolically.

Crucially, however, the novel does not remain at fractured confrontation. Its completed movement develops a way of inhabiting those contradictions without abolishing them. Pierre's eventual orientation toward ordinary life, relational attachment, finitude, and meaning does not prove suffering, death, contingency, or historical opacity unreal. Likewise, the historical sections do not restore sovereign human agency. The contradiction remains, but a coherent orientation becomes possible within it. That is the defining operation of 3c under the protocol: preserving existential fracture while integrating it into a coherent orientation rather than merely resolving it. 

### Boundary analysis

**Why 3c rather than 3b:** 3b would be sufficient if the dominant achievement terminated in confrontation with irreducible alienation, mortality, failed meaning, or the incompatibility of conscious intention with reality. *War and Peace* goes further. The completed trajectories of Pierre and, differently, Andrei convert those confrontations into an orientation toward existence in which uncertainty, suffering, limitation, and non-sovereign agency remain real. The protocol explicitly distinguishes integration from mere consolation or closure: contradiction must remain operative while consciousness finds a coherent way of containing it.  The novel meets that stronger condition.

Symbolic organization alone is consequently insufficient. The battlefield, domestic sphere, aristocratic society, religious experience, historical essays, and character transformations do not merely form an elaborate symbolic system; together they recursively interrogate why conscious representations of control, identity, causation, purpose, and history repeatedly fail against experienced reality. That clears the protocol's Level-3 requirement that symbolism cease to be sufficient and that recursion become globally operative. 

**Why not G:** there is a serious G candidate inside the work: its account of historical causation. *War and Peace* repeatedly displaces intentionalist explanations—especially explanations centered on commanders, plans, and supposedly decisive individual wills—with an account in which historical events arise from enormous interacting causal fields that exceed any participant's conscious control.

But the protocol requires more than a powerful explanatory theory. G requires **explanatory relocation to be the dominant operation of the completed artifact**, such that Levels 1–3 become insufficient to explain the work. A candidate mechanism must generate explanatory consequences for an independently identifiable target rather than merely provide an encompassing picture.  

The independent target can indeed be stated—**historical causation and the relationship between individual agency and large-scale events**—and the novel's distributed-causation model does explanatory work upon it. The failure occurs at **dominance/replacement**, not at mere absence of a candidate structure. Remove the explicit philosophy of history and its associated historical-causal mechanism, and a very large proportion of the completed novel's governing operation remains intelligible through its recursive-existential structure: Pierre, Andrei, Natasha, death, captivity, family life, moral striving, disillusionment, and the accommodation of consciousness to contingency. Conversely, removing the movement from existential fracture toward integration would fundamentally alter the completed work's trajectory.

Thus G describes an important local or subordinate operation, but **3c remains the lowest sufficient explanation of the artifact as a whole**. The protocol specifically forbids promoting an artifact merely because it contains a large explanatory structure or unusually profound generative-looking ideas.

## 2. Cognitive Magnitude Score

The dimensions are scored independently before CMS calculation, as required. The protocol uses integer anchors with evidence-grounded decimal interpolation and prohibits tuning dimensions to obtain a desired aggregate. 

### RL — Relational Load: **9.1**

**Principal evidence:** the novel simultaneously coordinates several families, intimate relationships, changing personal psychologies, social hierarchies, military organizations, battlefield events, political circumstances, historical causation, philosophical interpretation, and long temporal arcs. Remote events materially alter local relationships: war transforms courtship, inheritance, family structure, personal identity, spiritual orientation, and subsequent interpersonal possibilities.

This fully satisfies **RL9**, where heterogeneous relations span substantially different levels or domains while remaining mutually consequential.  There is limited movement beyond 9 because the relational span is extraordinary, but not enough for a stronger ceiling claim: significant portions remain locally or episodically decomposable, and sheer cast size is explicitly insufficient for extreme RL. The protocol requires both extreme interdependence and exceptional span for RL10. 

### AD — Abstraction Depth: **8.7**

**Principal evidence:** concrete events are repeatedly lifted through successive explanatory levels: individual action → intention and self-conception → social structure → collective behavior → historical causation → freedom and necessity → the epistemic limits of historical explanation. The novel repeatedly returns from those abstractions to concrete cases, testing the correspondence.

This clearly satisfies **AD8**, requiring deep constrained abstraction across substantial conceptual distance while preserving structural correspondence. It approaches AD9 because broad heterogeneous material is organized by meta-level questions about agency, causation, historical explanation, and consciousness. But the abstraction architecture is not consistently the dominant organizing structure of all the heterogeneous material at the unusually disciplined, invariant level required for complete AD9. 

### CD — Constraint Density: **8.4**

**Principal evidence:** the successful construction must simultaneously preserve psychological continuity across long character arcs, relational causality among families, historical chronology, military plausibility, shifts between intimate and mass scales, philosophical argument, thematic recurrence, and the compatibility of individual events with the work's critique of intentional historical causation.

That clearly establishes **CD8**: heterogeneous requirements interact across levels so that optimizing one element can compromise others. But CD9 requires numerous strongly coupled constraints to leave a **very narrow class of successful constructions**.  The novel does not demonstrate that degree of solution-space compression. Numerous episodes, subsidiary characters, descriptive passages, and local realizations could plausibly vary while preserving the principal achievement. The protocol explicitly warns against treating the complexity of a huge narrative as CD9–10 without evidence of such narrowness. 

### ID — Inferential Distance: **8.3**

**Principal evidence:** several governing relations are markedly non-routine reconstructions: apparent command versus actual collective causation; deliberate pursuit of meaning versus meaning emerging through lived participation; heroic agency versus contingent systems; individual subjective certainty versus historical unknowability.

The artifact clears **ID8**, which requires highly non-obvious restructuring with meaningful alternatives and no routine route.  It moves toward 9 because the successful synthesis could have taken numerous plausible alternative forms. I do not award ID9 because artifact evidence alone does not establish the stronger protocol claim that rich relevant information still leaves the central relation *deeply underdetermined* even for a strong reasoner. Furthermore, the enormous number of construction steps cannot itself increase ID: the protocol explicitly discounts accumulated routine complexity. 

### IC — Integrative Compression: **8.3**

**Principal evidence:** an enormous heterogeneous field is repeatedly organized by a comparatively small family of structural relations: intention versus outcome; abstraction versus lived experience; individual agency versus collective causation; egoic striving versus participation; separation versus relation; and the possibility of coherent life under contingency.

Those structures allow military history, domestic life, philosophical exposition, spiritual crisis, courtship, death, politics, and family formation to illuminate one another rather than remaining separate inventories. This fully establishes **IC8**, where a comparatively compact architecture organizes a large heterogeneous field with strong preservation and considerable reconstructive power. 

It does not reach IC9 because too much complexity remains independently represented rather than recoverable from a remarkably economical underlying architecture. The novel's vastness is neither a penalty nor a bonus: the protocol makes length neutral and demands economy relative to integrated load. 

### GR — Generalization Reach: **7.5**

**Principal evidence:** several core structures transfer beyond the immediate Napoleonic/Russian setting: discrepancies between planned action and emergent outcomes transfer to other collective systems; conflicts between abstract self-conception and lived reality transfer across social and personal contexts; its model of existential accommodation transfers among substantially different forms of loss, failure, mortality, family relation, and moral uncertainty.

That establishes **GR7**, under which the same architecture transfers reliably across substantially varied cases with identifiable boundaries. It moves meaningfully toward GR8 because these relations survive marked contextual changes with limited reconstruction. 

I stop below GR8 because most demonstrated transfer remains within human social, historical, psychological, and existential problem-spaces. The protocol explicitly warns that broad relevance to human experience cannot be treated automatically as transfer across markedly heterogeneous problem-spaces.
